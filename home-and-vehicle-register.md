# Home And Vehicle Register

One spreadsheet tab listing every appliance, system and vehicle we own that has a warranty, a service interval or a manual. If it costs more than $300 to replace, it is in here.

## Summary

Thirty-one rows as of 2026-10-19. Columns: item, location, brand and model, purchase date, price, installer, warranty end, service interval, expected life, manual link, Paperless document ID for the receipt. Serial numbers are in the sheet because service lines ask for them; no account numbers or logins.
