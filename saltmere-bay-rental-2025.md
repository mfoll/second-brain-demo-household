# Saltmere Bay Rental 2025

Rental for our week at Saltmere Bay, 2025-08-02 to 2025-08-09, booked 2025-03-10.

- Two-bedroom cottage four blocks from the beach, $1,610 for seven nights plus $150 cleaning.
- Owner rents directly; repeat guests get 10% off.
- Off-season rate (March) quoted at $160 a night with a three-night minimum.
- Total trip cost $3,120 including fuel; we drove (five hours).

First trip with Mom staying at Claire's. Kept as the baseline for [[spring-break-2027]].
