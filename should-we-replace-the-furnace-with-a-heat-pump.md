# Should We Replace The Furnace With A Heat Pump?

The 2007 gas furnace still works. The technician suggested planning replacement in three to five years ([[furnace-service-report-2025-10]]). Do we replace it with a heat pump on our schedule, and when?

## Current Answer

Leaning yes, but not before 2027-07. No money until the [[emergency-fund-rebuild]] is done.

## What We Know

- The 200-amp panel now has room for it ([[panel-upgrade-change-order-2026-08-12]]).
- Our winters are cold enough that a heat pump alone may struggle on the coldest days; a hybrid (heat pump plus keeping the gas furnace as backup) is the likely design. That also means keeping a gas bill.
- The national home energy credit is 30% capped at $2,000 a year, so attic insulation in one tax year and the heat pump in another claims more ([[heat-pump-payback]]).
- Attic insulation first would reduce the size needed ([[building-insulation]]).

## Missing

- Two installer quotes with a heat load calculation, not a rule of thumb.
- Attic insulation depth (fall checklist item).
- What the AC (2012) has left; replacing both together is the usual advice.

## Not Doing

Not replacing a working furnace in a panic. The point is to avoid a January failure decision like the water heater in May 2025.
