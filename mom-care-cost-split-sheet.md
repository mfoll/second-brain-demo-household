# Mom Care Cost Split Sheet

Shared spreadsheet (Nathan edits, Claire and Mom view) tracking every paid care cost and who owes what. One tab per year.

## Key Details

- Columns: date, payee, item, amount, split rule, Mom, Nathan, Claire, paid by, settled.
- Split rules: "aide" 60/20/20 (Mom, Nathan, Claire); "pendant" 60/20/20; "one-off at Mom's house" 100% Mom unless agreed otherwise; "assessment" 50/50 Nathan and Claire.
- Nathan pays Greywillow and the pendant service, then collects from Mom (monthly transfer) and Claire (quarterly).
- Q3 2026 settled 2026-10-05: aide $2,080 (16 visits in August and September, two of them 4.5 hours), pendant $126.

## Why It Matters

Keeps the money conversation boring, which was the point. The totals feed [[care-costs-to-date-2026]].

## Caveats

No account numbers; transfers are recorded as "transfer" with a date.
