# Brackwater Dental And Vision Plans 2027

## Summary

The dental and vision section of Brackwater's 2027 enrollment materials. Same plans as 2026; family premiums up $2 and $1 a month.

## Key Details

- Dental: one plan with a preferred network; two cleanings a year per person covered in full; family annual maximum unchanged. Orthodontics covered at 50% to a lifetime cap per child; nobody needs it now.
- Vision: one plan; annual eye exam with a copay, glasses or contacts allowance every 12 months.
- Family premium share: dental $58 a month, vision $14 a month.
- [[hana-kovar]] remains in the dental network.

## Why It Matters

Confirms nothing about dental or vision needed to change for 2027 ([[open-enrollment-review-2026-10-21]]).
