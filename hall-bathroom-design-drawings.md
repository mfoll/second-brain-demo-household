# Hall Bathroom Design Drawings

## Summary

Revision 2 of the hall bathroom drawings from Dana Whitlock, 2025-11-25, after the [[bathroom-design-meeting-2025-11-18]]. Plan and two elevations.

## Key Details

- Shower 60 by 36 inches, curbless, 36-inch [[linear-drain]] at the back wall, fixed glass panel, no door.
- Door widened from 28 to 34 inches; 32 inches clear.
- Blocking zones shaded on every wall at 33 to 36 inches and next to the toilet ([[wall-blocking]]).
- Vanity with knee space on one side; mirror lowered by 4 inches.
