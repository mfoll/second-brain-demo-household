# Assisted living

## Summary

Defines assisted living residences as group housing for adults who cannot or prefer not to live alone, close to a retirement home in feel and aimed mostly at older adults. Discusses the sector's growth as a consumer-driven business.

## Why It Matters

Nathan read it after Claire sent the Wrenhollow brochure ([[claire-email-assisted-living-case-2026-07]]), to understand what Wrenhollow actually offers and what it does not (it is not a nursing home). Useful framing for the C option in [[should-mom-stay-in-her-house]].

## Caveats

The business-model sections are general and partly opinionated; nothing in it tells us about Wrenhollow itself.
