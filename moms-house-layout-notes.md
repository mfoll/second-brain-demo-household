# Mom's House Layout Notes

Nathan's measured notes on Mom's house on Ashby Road, 2026-09, for the housing decision.

- 1964 two-story, three bedrooms, about 1,450 sq ft. Basement with laundry.
- Ground floor: living room, dining room, kitchen, half bath (toilet and basin, 30-inch door).
- Upstairs: three bedrooms, one full bathroom with tub and shower over.
- Stairs: straight, 14 risers at about 7.5 in, 36 in wide, now with rails both sides. Top landing 34 in deep.
- Basement stairs: 12 risers, steep, one rail. Laundry is down there.

## Options considered and set aside

- Bedroom in the dining room: possible, but no full bathroom downstairs. Adding a shower to the half bath means moving a wall into the kitchen; rough guess $18,000 to $25,000. Not pursued.
- Moving the washer and dryer to the kitchen: possible, about $2,500. Worth raising at the review; it would take the basement stairs out of her week.
