# Credit Union Rate Sheet 2026-07

## Summary

Pellbrook Community Credit Union's consumer rate sheet for July 2026, picked up from [[ruben-castillo]] when the rebuild started.

## Key Details

- Regular savings: 0.6% a year. High-yield savings for balances over $10,000: 3.1%. Our fund sits in regular savings, which is the obvious thing to fix.
- 12-month certificate: 3.6%, with a penalty for early withdrawal.
- HELOC: variable, currently 8.25%.
- Used auto loan, up to 5 years old, 48 months: 6.4% with automatic payment.

## Why It Matters

- The HELOC rate against the savings rate is the whole of [[should-we-pay-down-the-heloc-before-rebuilding-the-fund]].
- The auto loan rate goes into the minivan comparison ([[should-maya-drive-the-old-minivan]]).
- We moved the fund to the high-yield account on 2026-07-10 once it was clear the balance would stay above $10,000.
