# Professional development

## Summary

Overview of professional development: learning tied to a career field, used to earn or keep professional credentials through coursework, conferences and informal learning.

## Why It Matters

Read when I opened the [[pe-license-renewal]] Project and found I had only 12 hours with a year to go. It frames the board's requirement as maintaining a credential, which is how I now plan the hours: 4 a quarter ([[professional-development-hours]]).

## Key Details

- Purpose: earn or maintain credentials such as licenses and certifications.
- Formats: formal courses, conferences, informal learning.

## Caveats

Generic across professions; the engineering board's own rules on what counts are in [[engineering-board-renewal-notice-2026]].
