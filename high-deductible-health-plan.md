# High-deductible health plan

## Summary

English Wikipedia article on high-deductible health plans: coverage that trades lower premiums for a larger deductible, designed to make members more cost-conscious, and the plan type that makes a member eligible for a health savings account. It treats them as a form of catastrophic coverage and notes that their growth has outpaced understanding of their effects.

## Why It Matters

I read it the week before the [[health-plan-decision-2025-11-10]] to check I understood what Brackwater's plan was designed to do. The framing as catastrophic coverage is the honest one: it is cheap in a quiet year and expensive in a busy one, which is exactly what happened in 2026 ([[open-enrollment-review-2026-10-21]]).

## Caveats

The article describes one national system. We used it for the general mechanics only; Brackwater's plan documents ([[brackwater-benefits-guide-2026]]) govern our actual terms.
