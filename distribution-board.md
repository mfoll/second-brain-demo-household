# Distribution board

## Summary

Article on the distribution board, what we call the electrical panel or breaker box: the enclosure that splits the incoming supply into branch circuits, each protected by a fuse or breaker, usually with a main switch and, in newer boards, residual-current protection.

## Why It Matters

Read the night before signing the panel change order, after [[lena-marsh]]'s load calculation ([[kitchen-electrical-load-calculation]]). It helped me follow her explanation of why the old 100-amp board was the limit, not any single circuit.

## Key Details

- Terminology differs by country (consumer unit, breaker panel, load center); the article uses the British and international terms.
- Nothing here sizes a service; the sizing came from Lena's calculation, not the article.
