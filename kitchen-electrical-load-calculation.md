# Kitchen Electrical Load Calculation

## Summary

[[lena-marsh]]'s one-page load calculation for the house with the new kitchen, 2026-08-10. Result: the existing 100-amp service would run at about 96% of capacity at calculated demand; she recommends 200 A.

## Key Details

- New loads: induction range (50 A circuit), dishwasher, microwave drawer, refrigerator.
- Existing large loads: heat pump water heater (30 A), electric dryer (30 A), central AC (2012).
- Alternative offered: a load-management device that cuts the water heater while the range is on, about $900 installed. Hector and I rejected it because a future heat pump for heating would have pushed the service over anyway.
- 200-amp panel leaves space for a heat pump, an EV charger and a spare circuit for the garage.
