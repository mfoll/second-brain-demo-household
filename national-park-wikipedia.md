# National park

## Summary

General article on national parks: nature reserves protected mainly for conservation because of outstanding natural, historical or cultural value, usually owned and managed by a national government and open to the public. Criteria differ from country to country.

## Why It Matters

Eli needed a source for a geography assignment on protected areas the spring before our trip, and we read it together; it ended up as background for [[halvane-peaks-trip-2026]]. The conservation focus explains why the park caps lodging and why the cabins sold out by noon on opening day.

## Key Details

- Public access is nearly universal, but governments restrict use to protect the area.
- In some countries regional rather than national authorities run them.
