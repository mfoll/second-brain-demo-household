# Marisol Vega

Appliance repair technician with the Kestova authorized service network for our area. Warranty work on the new range, dishwasher and refrigerator goes through her.

First visit 2026-10-19: the refrigerator was delivered with the ice maker water line kinked behind the unit. She fixed it under warranty, levelled the unit and checked the dishwasher drain loop the installers had left too low. No charge. She recommended registering all three appliances online within 30 days of delivery so the warranty starts from the delivery date, not the manufacture date, which is now a step in [[log-a-new-appliance-or-warranty]].

She also told us the range's cooktop glass is not covered for impact damage under any warranty, extended or not. That fed [[do-we-buy-extended-warranties-on-the-kestova-appliances]].

Booking is through Kestova's service line, which asks for the model and serial numbers; those are in the [[home-and-vehicle-register]].
