# Interval training

## Summary

Describes interval training: repeated high-intensity efforts separated by easier recovery periods, which lets a runner spend more total time at high effort than one continuous hard run would.

## Why It Matters

[[calvin-odom]] insisted on one interval session a week; I read this before agreeing to give up a Tuesday easy run for it. Tuesday intervals became the fixed point of [[half-marathon-training-plan-v2]] and are one of the two things I would keep ([[pellbrook-river-half-2026-05-03]]).

## Key Details

- High-intensity work periods alternated with low-intensity recovery.
- Allows longer total time at higher intensity.

## Caveats

General exercise description, not a plan. Our sessions (6 x 800 m building to 8 x 800 m) were Calvin's.
