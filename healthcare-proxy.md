# Healthcare proxy

## Summary

Describes a healthcare proxy as a document naming an agent to make healthcare decisions for a person who cannot make them, comparable to a springing healthcare power of attorney.

## Why It Matters

Read alongside [[power-of-attorney]] before the meeting with [[harold-iwasaki]]. It made clear that the proxy only takes effect when Mom cannot decide, unlike the financial power of attorney she chose to make immediate. Nathan is the agent, Claire the successor ([[who-acts-for-mom-if-nathan-cannot]]).

## Caveats

Somewhat legalistic; terms differ by jurisdiction.
