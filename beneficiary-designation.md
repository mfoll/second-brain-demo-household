# Beneficiary Designation

A named beneficiary on an account or policy (retirement plans, life insurance, the HSA) that says who receives it at death. It passes directly to that person, outside the will, and generally overrides what the will says.

## Why it matters for us

- A perfect will does nothing for an account whose designation is wrong. In 2025-07 two of ours were: a blank contingent beneficiary on my retirement plan, and Renata's life insurance still naming her mother as contingent ([[beneficiary-designations-checklist]]).
- When the new wills create a trust for the children, contingent designations may need to name the trust rather than the children directly, so a minor does not receive money outright. Harold Iwasaki will advise on wording.

## Rule we follow

Review every designation whenever the wills change, a new account opens, or someone in the chain changes circumstances. Record only who is named by role, never account numbers.
