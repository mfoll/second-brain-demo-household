# Roof Inspection Notes 2026-09

Notes from a fifteen-minute look at the roof by the roofer Hector uses, 2026-09-12, while he was up for the new kitchen exhaust vent. Not a formal inspection; no charge.

- Original 1998 three-tab [[asphalt-shingle]] roof. Expected life "about 25 years, you're past it".
- South slope: curling at tab edges, heavy granule loss (visible in the gutters).
- North slope: better, some moss near the chimney.
- Chimney flashing resealed by a previous owner; looks sound.
- Two plumbing vent boots cracked; he replaced them for $60 on the spot.
- Decking felt solid where he walked. No sign of leaks in the attic (I checked the next day with a flashlight).
- Ballpark tear-off and architectural shingles: $14,000 to $19,000 depending on decking repairs.

Basis for [[roof-replacement]] and [[when-should-we-replace-the-roof]].
