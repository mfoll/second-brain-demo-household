# Portal Proxy Access

Access to another person's [[patient-portal]] account granted by that person through the clinic, at a level they choose, using the proxy's own login.

What it is not:

- Not a [[power-of-attorney]] or [[healthcare-proxy]]. Those give legal authority to act or decide. Portal proxy access gives a view and a channel, nothing else. Nathan had portal access three months before the legal documents were signed ([[portal-proxy-access-granted-2025-04-22]]).
- Not password sharing. Using Mom's own password would make every message look like hers and would break if she changed it.

Why it matters to us: it lets Nathan do the admin Mom asks for (appointments, bills, messages) while the records stay in the portal. It is the practical mechanism behind [[sensitive-records-stay-at-source]]. Mom chose summaries-level access and can revoke it at any time ([[portal-proxy-access-form-2025-04]]).
