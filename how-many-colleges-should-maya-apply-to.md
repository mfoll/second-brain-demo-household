# How Many Colleges Should Maya Apply To?

## Current Answer

Eight: two likely, four match, two reach. Agreed with [[janelle-forbes]] at the [[senior-counselor-meeting-2026-09-15]].

## Reasoning

- Maya's spring draft had eleven. Fees alone would have been about $780; the real cost was supplemental essays: eleven schools meant 14 extra essays, eight means 9.
- With balanced tiers ([[college-list-tiers]]), extra applications add little chance of a better outcome; Janelle sees most students admitted to at least one match when the list is built like this.
- Each application also means a college-specific aid form for most schools, which is Renata's and my work.

## Kept in Mind

- If both early action results in December are deferrals, Maya may add one more likely school before 2027-01-15. That was her condition for agreeing to eight.
