# Which Countertop Material?

Answered 2026-06-04: quartz.

| Material | Price for 46 sq ft | Upkeep | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quartz | $4,350 | None | Inside Quillan's line; Renata's pick |
| Granite | $4,100 | Seal yearly | Slab choice trip needed |
