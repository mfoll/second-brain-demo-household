# Maya's Driving Agreement 2026-03-22

Sunday kitchen-table meeting (well, folding table: the kitchen was not yet torn out but the hall bath was) with Maya, Renata and me to agree the house rules for her provisional license. Took 40 minutes. Result: [[house-driving-agreement]], signed by all three.

Agreed:

- Phone in the glovebox or in do-not-disturb driving mode while the car moves; navigation set before leaving. Background: [[distracted-driving]].
- Location sharing on while driving, off otherwise. Maya asked for this limit and we agreed.
- Legal provisional limits apply as written; our own extra: no highway alone until 2026-06.
- She pays $30 a month toward the insurance increase ([[teens-pay-a-share-of-their-costs]]).
- She fills the tank when it is below a quarter and logs it in the car's fuel note.
- Any ticket or scrape: tell us the same day; she pays a ticket, we handle insurance.

Not agreed, left open: whether she can drive friends to the swim meets out of town. Revisit after the season.

Review on 2027-03-18 when the provisional period ends.
