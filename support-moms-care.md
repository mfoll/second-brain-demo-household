# Support Mom's Care

Everything the family does so that Mom can keep living the way she chooses: the help she gets at home, her legal documents, the safety of her house, the people around her and the decisions about where she lives. It has no end date. It changes shape as her needs change, and it is shared with Claire, who pays a fifth of the paid help and wants a different long-term answer.
