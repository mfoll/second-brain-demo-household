# HDHP Is The Default For A Healthy Family

Superseded 2026-10-21 by [[hdhp-needs-a-full-emergency-fund]]. Kept as the record of what I believed when we switched.

## Stance

For a family with low, steady claims, the high-deductible plan with an HSA should be the default. The premium saving and the employer seed beat the copay plan in almost every year, and the HSA adds a tax-advantaged savings account on top.

## Why I Thought This

- 2024 and 2025 claims were both under $1,500.
- The comparison showed the HDHP cheaper in every scenario ([[health-plan-comparison-2026]]).
- The emergency fund was $26,000, enough to absorb a deductible.

## What Changed

The reasoning about cost held up. What it missed was timing: the deductible came due in one quarter, the same summer as the renovation overrun, and the fund it relied on was being drawn for something else. "Healthy family" also turned out to be the wrong frame; an injury is not a health trend, and teenagers on sports teams have them.
