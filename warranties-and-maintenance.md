# Warranties And Maintenance

Retrieval surface for keeping things running: when each appliance or car was bought, when its warranty ends, what service it needs and who does it.

## How we touch it

The [[home-and-vehicle-register]] is the single list. New items go in with [[log-a-new-appliance-or-warranty]]; the house gets the [[seasonal-house-maintenance-checklist]] in April and October. Receipts and warranty cards are scanned into Paperless-ngx and cited by document ID ([[household-document-archive]]).

## Key ideas

- [[service-interval]]: the register stores the interval, not the next date, and the calendar reminder holds the date.
- [[total-cost-of-ownership]]: how we compare repair against replace for cars and appliances.
- [[extended-warranty]], [[tire-rotation]] and [[heat-pump]] for background.
