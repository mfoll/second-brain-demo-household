Retrieval surface for how we support Mom and, later, how we want to be supported ourselves: paid and unpaid help, day programs, assisted living, home safety, and the load all of it puts on the family. The work itself lives under [[support-moms-care]]; this Topic gathers the reading and ideas around it.

## Key ideas

- [[aging-in-place]] and its limits; the [[care-ladder]] of steps between living alone and assisted living.

- [[dignity-of-risk]]: Mom's choices are hers, including ones we would not make.
- [[trial-with-review-date]]: how we try a change without arguing about it forever.
