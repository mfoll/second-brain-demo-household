# Total Cost Of Ownership

Comparing options by everything they cost over a fixed period, not by the price today: purchase, financing, fuel or energy, repairs, insurance, minus what the thing is worth at the end.

How I use it at home:

- Pick a horizon that matches the decision: five years for a car, ten for an appliance, fifteen for a furnace.
- Use our own history where we have it ([[minivan-service-history]], [[water-heater-energy-use-comparison]]) and say where it is a guess.
- Show the timing too. Two options with the same five-year total can feel very different if one needs $23,000 this year. That was Renata's point in [[should-maya-drive-the-old-minivan]], and she is right that the model hides it unless the cash flow is shown by year.
- Safety and convenience do not fit in the dollars. I list them next to the table rather than pretend to price them.

Also used for [[heat-pump-payback]] and in deciding against extended warranties ([[skip-extended-warranties]]).
