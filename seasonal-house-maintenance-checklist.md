# Seasonal House Maintenance Checklist

Run twice a year: the last weekend of April and the third weekend of October. Takes about half a day. Eli helps with the outside items for $20.

## Spring

1. AC service booked (every April).
2. Gutters and downspouts cleared; check downspouts drain away from the foundation.
3. Walk the roof line from the ground with binoculars; note shingle changes since fall.
4. Outdoor hose bibs on, check for freeze damage.
5. Sump pump test with a bucket.
6. Water heater: clean the air filter, check the condensate line.
7. Smoke and CO alarm test; batteries if not sealed units.

## Fall

1. Furnace service booked (every October, [[furnace-service-report-2025-10]]).
2. Gutters cleared after the leaves drop.
3. Hose bibs off and drained from inside.
4. Attic look with a flashlight: any stains, insulation depth (not yet measured; see [[home-energy-retrofits]]).
5. Dryer vent cleaned.
6. Water heater filter and anode check.
7. Register review: warranty ends in the next six months and expected-life items ([[home-and-vehicle-register]]).

## Record

Note the date done and anything found in the [[run-the-house]] maintenance log, one line. Problems get their own note or Question.
