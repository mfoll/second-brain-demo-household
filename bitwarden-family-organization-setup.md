# Bitwarden Family Organization Setup

How the family Bitwarden organization is arranged, set up in February 2025 following [[bitwarden-organizations-overview]]. No credentials or item names here, only structure.

## Members

- Nathan and Renata: owners.
- Maya: member since 2025-09, "Kids" collection only, plus her own personal vault.
- Claire: member, "Mom" collection only, so she can reach Mom's care contacts and the clinic portal details when she is covering ([[moms-care-documents-index]]).
- Eli: not yet; planned for when he gets his learner's permit in 2027.

## Collections

| Collection | Holds | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Money | Bank, credit union, tax authority, retirement logins; account numbers | Owners |
| Insurance | Policy numbers, claims lines, ID card images, current declarations | Owners |
| Health | Plan member IDs and card images, patient portal logins | Owners |
| House | Alarm codes, Wi-Fi, utility accounts, appliance accounts, the fire-safe combination | Owners |
| Kids | School portal, activity registrations | Owners, Maya |
| Mom | Mom's portal proxy login, care contacts | Owners, Claire |

## Emergency access

Renata and I are each other's emergency contact with takeover access after 7 days. Claire has view-only access to my account after 14 days. Set up 2025-07 after the will consultation ([[bitwarden-about-emergency-access]]).

## Rules

- Anything that would let someone act as us goes here, not in Paperless-ngx or the vault.
- When a card or policy changes, update the item and delete the old attachment the same day.
