# Process A School Form Or Fee

How we handle a permission slip, an activity fee or any form from Pellbrook High, a coach or a club, so it gets done once and can be found later. Written 2025-09-07 after we paid the swim fee twice.

## When to use

Any school or activity request that needs a signature, a payment or a decision from a parent.

## Steps

1. **Capture.** Whoever sees it first forwards the email or photographs the paper to the family inbox. Note the due date.
2. **Decide who.** Renata does payments; I do forms and signatures unless I am travelling. Kids may fill in their own parts.
3. **Check the budget line.** Fees go against the "Kids: activities and school" line in the [[household-budget-spreadsheet]]. Anything over $100 not already on [[kids-activity-costs-2026-27]] is discussed at the next Sunday calendar check.
4. **Do it.** Most fees and forms are in the school parent portal now; login in Bitwarden. Pay by card, never by cash in an envelope.
5. **File.** Save the receipt or signed form to Paperless-ngx with tag "School 2026-27" and the kid's name as correspondent ([[household-document-archive]]).
6. **Record.** Add the amount and Paperless ID to [[kids-activity-costs-2026-27]]. For a form with a date (a trip, a competition), add the date to the [[family-calendar]].

## Guardrails

- Health forms (sports physicals, medication permissions) are filed in Paperless-ngx only. The vault records "done" and the date, never the content ([[kids-notes-stay-logistical]]).
- Emergency contact forms: check who is listed every September ([[school-emergency-contact-forms-2026]]).

## Verification

The fee or form shows in the portal as complete, and the row exists in the cost note.
