# Paperless Tagging Scheme

The tags and correspondents we use in Paperless-ngx, so any document can be found in two clicks and vault notes can cite it by ID.

## Tags

- Area: `house`, `car`, `insurance`, `health`, `legal`, `school`, `mom`, `budget`, `warranty`.
- Year-bound: `tax-YYYY` for anything the return for that year needs. Applied on arrival, not in February.
- Person: `nathan`, `renata`, `maya`, `eli`, only when the document is about one person.
- State: `hsa-unreimbursed`, `hsa-reimbursed` for eligible medical receipts ([[should-we-save-hsa-receipts-for-later-reimbursement]]); `inbox` until processed.

## Correspondents

One per organization: Merriline Mutual, Brackwater Utilities, Tessaline Health, Pellbrook Community Credit Union, National Revenue Office, Quillan Remodeling, Pellbrook High School, and so on.

## Permissions

Owner accounts see everything. Documents tagged `legal` and the old wills are restricted to the two owner accounts. Claire has a read-only account limited to `mom`.

## Rules

- Title format: `YYYY-MM-DD correspondent short description`.
- Never type an identifier into a title or note; Paperless's full-text index already has it.
- Shred paper after scanning unless it is an original listed for the fire safe ([[household-document-archive]]).
