# Skip Extended Warranties

## Stance

We do not buy extended warranties on appliances or electronics. We register the maker's warranty, keep receipts findable, and self-insure the rest through the emergency fund.

## Scope

Items under about $2,500 each. A car's powertrain plan or a whole-house system might be different and gets its own look.

## Why I Think This

- Plans are priced so the seller wins on average ([[extended-warranty]]). We have enough savings to absorb one repair.
- Many overlap the maker's warranty, so the real extra cover is shorter than advertised.
- Our repair history: three appliance repairs in fifteen years, about $560 total ([[home-and-vehicle-register]]).
- The Kestova plan excluded the most likely expensive damage anyway ([[kestova-range-manual]]).

## What Would Change My Mind

An emergency fund too small to cover a $1,500 repair, which is close to where we are in 2026 ([[emergency-fund-rebuild]]); or a product with a known failure pattern and a fairly priced plan. Renata thinks the first point should have made us buy this time. I disagree, but it is why confidence is Medium.

## Implications

Every new item goes through [[log-a-new-appliance-or-warranty]] within 30 days.
