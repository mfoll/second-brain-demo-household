# Deductible

## Summary

English Wikipedia article on the deductible: the amount a policyholder pays before the insurer pays anything, and the different clause types insurers use as a payment threshold (fixed amounts, percentages, per-claim and aggregate forms).

## Why It Matters

Read during the [[household-budget-reset]] when we went through every policy's deductibles at once for the first time. The distinction between a per-claim deductible (car, home) and an annual aggregate one (health) is the reason our health plan needs its own cash planning. It also underpinned dropping collision on the Tessera ([[should-we-drop-collision-on-the-tessera]]) and later the [[health-plan-break-even]] sheet.

## Key Details

- Per-claim deductibles reset with each claim; the health plan's resets each calendar year.
- Higher deductibles lower premiums; whether that pays depends on how often you claim and on the cash you hold.
