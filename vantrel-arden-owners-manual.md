# Vantrel Arden Owner's Manual

Capture from the 2021 Arden manual, read after the transmission quote to compare what the two cars do for a new driver.

- Forward collision warning with automatic braking, standard; works from about 5 mph; can be turned off but resets at each start.
- Lane departure warning, blind-spot warning, rear cross-traffic alert.
- A "teen driver" key setting: speed alert and volume limit. We have not used it.
- Hybrid battery warranty to 2029-04.

The Tessera has rear camera only. This is the factual basis for the middle option in [[should-maya-drive-the-old-minivan]] where Maya drives the Arden.
