# Emergency fund

## Summary

English Wikipedia article on emergency funds (also called contingency funds): money set aside for unexpected costs so a household does not have to borrow or run out of cash when something goes wrong. It treats the fund as a basic part of financial planning.

## Why It Matters

Re-read on the day the [[emergency-fund-rebuild]] started, after the first renovation change order. The point that stuck: the fund exists so you do not have to take on debt in a bad month. That is the argument Renata used for drawing on the fund rather than the HELOC ([[how-should-we-fund-the-renovation-overrun]]), and it is the reason the fund now has to be rebuilt rather than left low.

## Caveats

The article does not settle how large a fund should be for a particular household; we sized ours in [[how-big-should-the-emergency-fund-be]].
