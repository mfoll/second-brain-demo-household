# Early admission

## Summary

Short article on early admission plans in general: students apply earlier than usual and hear earlier, which spreads out their workload and gives colleges an early view of their incoming class. It gives typical deadlines of mid-October or the start of November, with results by mid-December.

## Why It Matters

Part of my first college reading in fall 2025, when Maya was in grade 11 and the terms were new to us. It is the umbrella term; the distinction that actually mattered is in [[early-action-vs-early-decision]].

## Key Details

- Benefits to students: fewer applications at once, early results.
- Benefits to colleges: early picture of the class.
- Deadlines around 2026-11-01 matched Maya's two early action schools.

## Caveats

Does not separate binding from non-binding plans clearly; read [[early-decision]] for that. Kept in [[college-planning]] for Eli.
