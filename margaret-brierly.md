# Margaret Brierly

Mom. 79, widowed since 2019, living alone in her own house on Ashby Road, ten minutes from us. Retired school librarian. Sings in the church choir, gardens from April to October, and has opinions about all of it.

## Role in the household

Mom is not a project; she is the person the [[moms-care-plan]] works for. She makes her own decisions. Nathan holds her [[power-of-attorney]] and [[healthcare-proxy]] ([[moms-legal-documents]]), with [[claire-dunmore]] as successor, and has proxy access to her clinic portal. Neither document has been used to decide anything for her, and the family intends to keep it that way as long as possible ([[mom-decides-where-she-lives]]).

## How we work together

- Nathan visits Tuesday evening and brings her to Sunday lunch at ours. Renata calls midweek. Maya and Eli visit on their own schedule, usually once a week between them.
- Mom prefers phone calls to texts, except with [[fern-ochoa]], with whom she trades a good-morning text every day.
- Decisions about her go through her first, then the family. She dislikes being discussed in the third person when she is in the room, and says so.
- She stays over at ours a few nights a year (holidays, after her January 2025 fall); the hall bathroom's curbless shower was designed partly for her ([[hall-bathroom-remodel]]).
