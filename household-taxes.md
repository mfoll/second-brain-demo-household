# Household Taxes

The annual household return to the National Revenue Office and the paperwork around it. [[imogen-park]] prepares it; I gather the documents and keep the record of what was claimed.

## What recurs

- January to February: wage statements, the credit union interest statement, mortgage interest, charitable receipts. Checklist in [[gather-tax-documents]].
- Credits we have claimed: the 30% home energy credit, capped at $2,000 a year, for the heat pump water heater ([[home-energy-credit-worksheet-2025]], [[tax-credit]]).
- From the 2026 return on: HSA contribution and withdrawal forms ([[health-savings-account]]).

## Returns

- [[tax-return-2024-summary]]: filed 2025-04-11, no credits.
- [[tax-return-2025]]: filed 2026-04-09, $735 energy credit, $1,280 refund ([[tax-return-2025-summary]]).

## Stance

We keep paying a professional while credits and the HSA are in play: [[pay-a-professional-for-the-tax-return]].

## Watch

- If we replace the furnace with a heat pump (still reading, under home energy retrofits), the same credit applies again, capped per year. Timing it across two tax years could matter.
