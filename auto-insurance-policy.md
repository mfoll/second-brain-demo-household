# Auto Insurance Policy

Summary of our Merriline Mutual auto policy as amended on 2026-03-20, when Maya was added as a driver. The policy number, the declarations page and the ID cards are in Bitwarden and Paperless-ngx; this note keeps coverage and premium history only.

## Summary

One policy covering both cars and three drivers, renewing each January, handled by [[ansel-duarte]]. Adding Maya after her license test on 2026-03-18 ([[maya-license-test-2026-03-18]]) raised the annual premium by $1,140 after the good-student discount ([[auto-policy-update-2026-03-20]]).

## Key Details

Vehicles:

| Vehicle | Coverage | Deductibles |
|---|---|---|
| 2014 Vantrel Tessera minivan (151,000 miles in 2026-08) | Liability, comprehensive; collision dropped in 2025-02 | Comprehensive $500 |
| 2021 Vantrel Arden hybrid sedan | Liability, comprehensive, collision | Comprehensive $500, collision $1,000 |

Liability limits sit at the level the [[umbrella-insurance-policy]] requires underneath it.

Drivers: Nathan, Renata, Maya (from 2026-03-20, listed as an occasional driver of both cars and principal driver of neither, which is accurate while she shares them).

Premium history (annual):

| Period | Premium | Change |
|---|---|---|
| 2025 policy year | $2,180 | Collision dropped on the Tessera in 2025-02 saved $310 ([[should-we-drop-collision-on-the-tessera]]) |
| 2026 renewal, January | $2,310 | General rate increase |
| From 2026-03-20, Maya added | $3,450 | +$1,140 a year after the good-student discount ([[merriline-good-student-discount-form]]) |

Without the good-student discount the increase would have been $1,520. The discount needs a school form each August showing Maya's grades meet the threshold; we only record that it was sent.

## Caveats

- The Tessera has no collision cover, which suits a car worth about $4,000 but matters if Maya has an at-fault crash in it. That cuts both ways in [[should-maya-drive-the-old-minivan]].
- Ansel's rough figure for a used hybrid SUV in place of the Tessera, with collision: about $380 a year more. Not a formal quote.
- Eli will be eligible for a learner's permit in 2027; expect another increase when he is licensed.
