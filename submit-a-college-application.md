# Submit A College Application

Checklist for each of Maya's eight applications, from "ready" to "confirmed". Maya does the application; this procedure is the family's side so that nothing falls between us. Written 2026-10-12, first used for the two early action schools.

## When to use

For each college on [[maya-college-list]], starting ten school days before its deadline.

## Inputs

- The college's row in [[maya-college-list]]: deadline, plan (early action or regular), fee, aid form deadline.
- Maya's finished application in her own account.
- Card for the fee (Bitwarden, family organization) and Renata.

## Steps

1. **Ten school days before.** Check in [[maya-college-list]] that the college is added in the portal, so [[janelle-forbes]] can send the transcript and school report. Maya confirms both teacher letters are assigned to that college ([[recommendation-letter-requests-2026]]).
2. **Seven days before.** Calendar reminder fires ([[family-calendar]]). Maya says whether the essays are final. If not, agree a date two days before the deadline.
3. **Submission day.** Maya submits; Renata pays the fee on the same screen. Do not submit on deadline day: portals slow down.
4. **Same day.** Save the confirmation page and fee receipt as PDF to Paperless-ngx, tag "College 2027", correspondent the college ([[household-document-archive]]). Update the row: submitted date, fee paid, Paperless document ID.
5. **Within a week.** The college sends a portal login by email. Maya creates it; the login goes into Bitwarden, collection "Maya college", which Maya and Renata can open.
6. **Within two weeks.** Check the college portal shows transcript, school report and both letters received. Chase missing items through Janelle, not the teachers.
7. **Aid.** If the college needs its own aid form, add its deadline to the calendar and follow [[student-aid-form-checklist]].

## Guardrails

- No essays, scores or grades into the vault or into Paperless-ngx. Confirmations and receipts only ([[kids-notes-stay-logistical]]).
- Never tick the binding early decision box ([[should-maya-apply-early-decision]]).
- The college login is Maya's; we hold a copy only so nothing is lost.

## Verification

Each row in [[maya-college-list]] has a submitted date, a fee receipt ID and "all documents received" before the procedure is done for that college.
