# Adult Day Program Lessons

What we would do differently, written 2026-07-05.

1. **Start from what Mom asked for.** She asked for help with the house. We offered company and activities, because that worried us.
2. **Set a review date.** No end date meant two months of attendance near zero before anyone said stop ([[care-decisions-need-a-written-review-date]]).
3. **Try one activity before a whole program.** The garden club would have been a better first step ([[pellbrook-senior-center-program-guide-2026]]).
4. **Watch the season.** Starting in March meant the program competed with her garden.
5. **Pay attention to "too easy".** Her first-day comment was a signal.

What went right: [[patrice-leclerc]] was honest early, the cost stayed low ($702), and Mom was never made to feel she had failed.
