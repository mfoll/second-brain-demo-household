# Hall Bathroom Remodel

The upstairs hall bathroom, rebuilt so that it works for Mom when she stays over and for us as we age. Done in twelve weeks.

## Outcome

Hall bathroom rebuilt with a curbless shower, linear drain, wider door and wall blocking for grab bars; inspection passed 2026-05-20.

## Current State

- Complete. Inspection passed 2026-05-20, final walk-through 2026-05-22.
- Two grab bars installed now (shower and toilet); blocking behind every wall for more later.

## Why A Curbless Shower

Asked often enough that it gets its own answer. At the [[bathroom-design-meeting-2025-11-18]] with [[hector-quillan]] and [[dana-whitlock]] we chose a curbless shower with a [[linear-drain]], a 34-inch door and [[wall-blocking]] in every wall, instead of a new tub. It added $4,200, inside the $71,400 contract. Reasons: Mom stays over several times a year and has been careful on stairs and tub edges since her January 2025 fall, and the house should work for Renata and me as we age ([[aging-in-place]], [[build-for-the-next-twenty-years]]). Renata first wanted a tub for resale; the compromise was keeping the tub in the primary bathroom ([[should-the-primary-bathroom-keep-its-tub]]).

## Working Context

- Floor recessed 3/4 inch between joists so the shower floor slopes to the drain with no curb. Sheet membrane over [[cement-board]] on the walls, liquid membrane on the floor, flood-tested 24 hours before tile.
- Tile by [[sione-tupou]]: 12x24 porcelain, slip-rated for wet floors.
- Kids used the primary bathroom for twelve weeks; the schedule is in the [[family-calendar]].
