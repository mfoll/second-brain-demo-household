# Respite care

## Summary

Explains respite care as planned or emergency short-term care that gives unpaid family caregivers a break, to protect the caregiver relationship. Mentions that it can also be a good experience for the person cared for.

## Why It Matters

Part of the reasoning for the [[adult-day-program-trial]]: two days a week of program would give Mom company and give Nathan two days without wondering. In hindsight we were solving our problem more than hers, which is one of the [[adult-day-program-lessons]].

## Caveats

Written mostly about caregivers of people with significant disability or cognitive loss; Mom's situation is lighter, so we borrowed the idea rather than the model.
