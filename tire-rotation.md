# Tire rotation

## Summary

Short article on tire rotation: moving each wheel and tire to a different position on the vehicle so the set wears evenly. It notes that how much this actually extends tire life is disputed.

## Why It Matters

[[joon-ho-bae]] rotates with every oil change at 7,500 miles. I looked it up when setting [[service-interval]]s in the [[home-and-vehicle-register]], wondering whether it was worth paying for separately. Since it rides along with the oil change at , we keep it.

## Key Details

- The benefit is debated, so I would not pay for an extra visit just for rotation.
- The Arden's hybrid manual specifies its own pattern; the register links it.
