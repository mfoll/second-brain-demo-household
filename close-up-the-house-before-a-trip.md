# Close Up The House Before A Trip

Checklist for any trip of three nights or more. Used for Saltmere Bay (2025) and Halvane Peaks (2026).

## One Week Before

1. Confirm Mom's arrangements with her and Claire; send both our itinerary and lodging phone numbers ([[where-should-mom-stay-during-the-halvane-trip]]).
2. Email [[bev-halloran]]: dates, our numbers, what to water, who to call for what (plumber [[ray-okonjo]], Hector if anything renovation-related). Template below.
3. Hold the mail if Bev cannot take it in.
4. Pay or schedule any bills due while away; check the [[family-calendar]].
5. Check the alarm call list is current ([[spare-key-and-alarm-contacts]]).

## Day Before

1. Empty the fridge of perishables; trash and recycling out.
2. Water heater to vacation mode (heat pump unit has a setting).
3. Thermostat: 60°F in winter, 82°F in summer.
4. Unplug the TV, router stays on (cameras).
5. Close and lock basement windows; check the sump pump runs by pouring in a bucket.

## Leaving

1. Main water valve off for trips over five nights (Bev knows to call if she needs it on for the garden).
2. Alarm on, all doors locked, garage door unplugged.
3. Text Bev and Claire "gone".

## Email Template For Bev

Dates, where we are, our two mobile numbers, the lodging landline, plants and what to water, "call Ray for water, call me for anything else", thank you.

## Verification

On return: walk the basement, check the sump and any water under the water heater, reset the thermostat and water heater, write the trip debrief.
