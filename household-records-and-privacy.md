# Household Records And Privacy

Retrieval surface for where the family's sensitive records live and how this vault refers to them without holding them. The rule is short: the vault says where something is, who can open it and what we decided. It never holds a diagnosis, a medication list, an account number, a policy number or a password.

## Where things live

- Medical records: each person's patient portal. Mom's portal at Wrenfield Family Clinic, with Nathan as proxy since 2025-04-22 ([[portal-proxy-access-granted-2025-04-22]]).
- Logins, policy numbers and insurance card images: the family Bitwarden organization.
- Scans: Paperless-ngx on the home server, cited here by document ID ([[household-document-archive]]).
- Originals of legal documents: the fire safe in the basement.
