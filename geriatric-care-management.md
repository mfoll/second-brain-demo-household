# Geriatric care management

## Summary

Describes geriatric care management as planning and coordinating long-term care for older people, working with them and their families to keep independence and quality of life, drawing on health and social care knowledge.

## Why It Matters

Found in 2025-02 when we did not know who to ask after Mom's fall. It is why we hired [[denise-farrow]] for an independent assessment instead of asking an agency that would also sell us the service ([[denise-farrow-home-assessment-2025-03-19]]).

## Caveats

Says little about how to choose or pay one.
