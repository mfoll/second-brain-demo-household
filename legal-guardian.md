# Legal guardian

## Summary

English Wikipedia article on legal guardians: people appointed by a court or otherwise given authority to make personal and financial decisions for someone who cannot, such as housing, medical care and money. It covers guardianship of adults with reduced capacity as well as of children.

## Why It Matters

Read with [[will-and-testament]] before the 2025 consultation. Two useful points for us: a guardian of a child decides about daily life, schooling and care, which is a different job from managing the money left to that child; and a court makes the final appointment, with the parents' nomination as strong guidance. We are treating those as two separate choices in [[guardianship-options-worksheet]].

## Caveats

The adult-guardianship material is not relevant to Mom, whose arrangements use a power of attorney and healthcare proxy instead ([[moms-legal-documents]]).
