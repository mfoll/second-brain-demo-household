# Out-Of-Pocket Maximum

The most a health plan member pays for covered care in a plan year: deductible, copays and coinsurance together. Once it is reached the plan pays everything else that is covered until the year resets. Premiums do not count toward it.

## Our numbers

| Plan | Family deductible | Family out-of-pocket maximum |
|---|---|---|
| Brackwater copay plan | $1,000 | $6,000 |
| Brackwater high-deductible plan | $4,000 | $8,000 |

## Why it matters

It is the number that bounds the worst case. In the comparison, worst-case cost = annual premium + out-of-pocket maximum − any employer seed. For us the HDHP's worst case ($10,840 for 2027) is still below the copay plan's ($13,800), which is why the 2027 decision turned on cash timing, not total cost ([[cash-timing-risk]], [[health-plan-comparison-2027]]).

## Watch

Out-of-network care does not count, and some plans have separate individual maximums inside the family one. Ours do: $4,000 per person on the HDHP.
