# Budgeting

How we plan and track household money: the zero-based monthly budget, sinking funds for lumpy costs, and the emergency fund.

## How we budget now

Every dollar of take-home pay gets a job at the start of the month in the [[household-budget-spreadsheet]]. Irregular costs (car repairs, travel, gifts, home maintenance, insurance renewals) get a monthly [[sinking-fund]] line so they stop ambushing us. The emergency fund sits on top and is sized in [[months-of-expenses]]. The method came out of the [[household-budget-reset]] in early 2025; the envelope-style sheet it replaced is archived as [[budget-spreadsheet-2024-envelopes]].

## Rhythm

- First Sunday of the month: [[monthly-money-meeting]] with Renata.
- Renewals and deadlines through the year: [[annual-money-calendar]].
