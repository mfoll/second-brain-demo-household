# Driver's education

## Summary

Overview of formal driver training: classes or programs that prepare a new driver for a learner's permit or license, delivered in a classroom, in a car, online or mixed. Typical content is traffic law, vehicle handling and hazards such as weather, road conditions and impaired driving.

## Why It Matters

Read while choosing between a course with in-car lessons and parent-only teaching. We chose the Wrenfield Driving School course with six in-car lessons ([[wrenfield-driving-school-enrollment]]) mainly so that Maya's first highway and parking sessions were with a professional ([[derek-mbeki]]).

## Key Details

- Formats range from classroom only to fully online to blended with in-car time.
- Topics listed include traffic code, vehicle operation and dangerous conditions.

## Caveats

Says nothing about whether courses improve outcomes for a given learner, and nothing specific to our licensing office. Renata pointed out the course mattered more for her peace of mind than for the test.
