# Tax credit

## Summary

English Wikipedia article on tax credits: amounts certain taxpayers can subtract directly from the tax they owe, as opposed to deductions that reduce taxable income. It also describes credits for tax already paid and compares a credit to a rebate.

## Why It Matters

I wanted to be sure the home energy credit was worth what I thought before chasing the paperwork. A credit comes off the bill dollar for dollar, so 30% of the $2,450 heat pump water heater is a straight $735 off our tax, not a smaller saving from a deduction. That was worth the extra page Imogen needed ([[home-energy-credit-worksheet-2025]]).

## Caveats

The national credit's rules (rate, $2,000 annual cap, qualifying equipment) come from the National Revenue Office guidance Imogen uses, not from this article.
