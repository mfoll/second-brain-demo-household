# Safety Features Before Age For Teen Cars

## Stance

My view: the car a new driver uses most should have automatic emergency braking, even if that means a newer, more expensive car than we would otherwise buy. Of all the factors (age, size, mileage), crash-avoidance features are what I would pay for first.

## Scope

The car Maya, and from 2027 Eli, drive most. Not a rule about our own cars.

## Why I Think This

New drivers make more following-distance and attention errors, which is exactly what forward collision warning with braking is designed for ([[collision-avoidance-system]]). The 2014 Tessera has none of it. Joon-ho said much the same unprompted, though as a parent, not as a mechanic ([[joon-ho-bae]]).

## What Would Change My Mind

- If the money really threatens the [[emergency-fund-rebuild]], which is Renata's argument.
- The middle option where Maya drives the 2021 Arden, which has the feature ([[vantrel-arden-owners-manual]]), would satisfy this stance without a purchase.
