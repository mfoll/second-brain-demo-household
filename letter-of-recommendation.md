# Letter of recommendation

## Summary

Describes recommendation letters: a document in which someone who has worked with or taught a person assesses their qualities and abilities, used for jobs, higher education admission and scholarships.

## Why It Matters

Looked up the day after the [[senior-counselor-meeting-2026-09-15]] because Maya needed to choose two teachers. The point we took: the writer should be someone who has taught her recently and in a subject close to what she wants to study. The requests themselves are tracked in [[recommendation-letter-requests-2026]].

## Key Details

- Written by a teacher, supervisor or colleague who knows the person's work.
- Used for employment, admission and scholarships.

## Caveats

General; nothing about the school's process or timing, which come from [[janelle-forbes]].
