# Caregiver burden

## Summary

Defines caregiver burden, or burnout, as the combined physical, emotional, social and financial strain on unpaid people looking after a relative or friend.

## Why It Matters

Renata sent it to Nathan in 2026-06, when he was doing Mom's shopping, two visits a week and the day program logistics while the kitchen was being demolished. It reframed the July call: the question was not only what Mom needs, but what the family can sustain ([[claire-and-nathan-care-roles]], [[family-care-call-2026-07-26]]).
