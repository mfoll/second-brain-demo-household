# Marrowfield Training Allowance Policy

My summary of the part of Marrowfield Engineering Group's staff handbook that covers paid training, read 2026-01-07 when planning the renewal hours.

## Summary

- $1,200 per engineer per calendar year for courses, webinars and conference fees related to the engineer's work or license.
- Unused allowance does not carry over.
- Travel for approved courses is paid separately from the project overhead budget.
- Training days count as work days if approved by the group lead at least two weeks ahead.
- License renewal fees are not covered; the engineer pays them.
