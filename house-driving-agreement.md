# House Driving Agreement

One-page agreement between Maya, Renata and me on how Maya drives our cars during her provisional year. Signed by all three on 2026-03-22 ([[maya-driving-agreement-2026-03-22]]). Eli will get an adapted version.

## Summary

Eight rules, the cost split and a review date.

1. Legal provisional limits apply: no driving 23:00 to 05:00 without a licensed adult, no more than one non-family passenger under 21, until 2027-03-18.
2. Phone in the glovebox or in driving mode; navigation set before the car moves ([[distracted-driving]]).
3. Location sharing on while driving.
4. No highway alone until 2026-06 (met; first solo highway drive 2026-06-14).
5. Ask before taking a car; the [[family-calendar]] shows who needs which car.
6. Fill the tank below a quarter; log it in the car's fuel note.
7. Any ticket, scrape or warning light: tell a parent the same day.
8. No driving after any alcohol, ever, regardless of the legal limit.

Costs: Maya pays $30 a month toward the insurance increase and any ticket she gets; we pay repairs and the rest of the insurance ([[teens-pay-a-share-of-their-costs]], [[auto-insurance-policy]]).

## Key Details

- Breaking rule 2, 7 or 8 means no car for two weeks; the agreement says so and Maya proposed the two weeks.
- Review on 2027-03-18 and whenever the car changes ([[should-maya-drive-the-old-minivan]]).

## Caveats

It is a family agreement, not a legal document. The provisional limits are summarized from the licensing office's handout ([[provisional-license-handout]]); the handout governs.
