# How Big Should The Emergency Fund Be?

Raised 2026-07-06 when the rebuild started; answered at the 2026-09 money meeting.

## Current Answer

> [!info]
> $28,000: four months of core expenses at about $7,000 a month. Not six months, because both of us are in stable jobs and we have the HELOC as a second line; not three, because 2026 showed two surprises can land in one summer.

## Reasoning

- Core expenses only, as [[mei-tanaka-ross]] recommended: mortgage, HELOC, utilities, groceries, insurance, our share of Mom's aide, transport, phones, the car, home and medical sinking funds and a minimum for the kids. See [[months-of-expenses]].
- 2026 drew $8,640 out in three months. Four months of cover leaves room for one more surprise during a rebuild.
- Six months ($42,000) would take until 2029 at the current transfer and starve the minivan and the wills.

## Revisit

When the HELOC is paid off (core expenses drop by $400), or when Maya's college costs start in 2027-08.
