# Home care

## Summary

Covers care delivered in the person's own home, from clinical care by professionals to help with daily living for older or disabled people, and notes that the scope depends on local rules.

## Why It Matters

Read before the [[family-care-call-2026-07-26]] to separate the two kinds of home help. What we want for Mom is the non-clinical kind: household help and company, no personal care. That distinction went straight into the service plan with Greywillow ([[greywillow-intake-visit-2026-07-29]]).

## Caveats

Broad article across many countries; regulation and terms vary.
