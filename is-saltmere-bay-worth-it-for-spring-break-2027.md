# Is Saltmere Bay Worth It For Spring Break 2027?

Three off-season nights at Saltmere Bay cost about $1,400. Is that worth it in a year when money is tight and Maya may need the time for college visits?

- For: everybody liked it in 2025; the cottage owner offers repeat guests 10% off; it is a five-hour drive, no flights.
- Against: March is cold on the coast; it uses most of the travel sinking fund; Maya may prefer an admitted-students day.
- Depends on: admission decisions (2027-02 to 03), the school calendar, and whether the minivan decision eats the spring budget.

Revisit at the 2027-02 money meeting.
