# Insurance

Everything about how we insure the family: the home, auto and umbrella policies with Merriline Mutual, the health, dental and vision plans through Brackwater, and the general question of how much risk we keep ourselves.

## Working stance

We insure the catastrophe and self-insure the repair: high deductibles where the savings are real, an umbrella for liability, no extended warranties. The one exception we made this year was health: see [[hdhp-needs-a-full-emergency-fund]]. Stance in full: [[insure-catastrophes-not-repairs]].

## Policies

- [[auto-insurance-policy]]: two cars, three drivers since Maya's license, $3,450 a year.
- [[home-insurance-policy]]: dwelling coverage raised for the renovation at the 2026 renewal ([[home-insurance-renewal-2026-06-01]]).
- [[umbrella-insurance-policy]]: $1 million over the home and auto liability limits.
- Health: [[health-plan-switch-2026]], [[open-enrollment-2027]]; overview of costs in [[insurance-overview-2026]].
