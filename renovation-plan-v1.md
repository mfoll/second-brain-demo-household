# Renovation Plan v1

First budget for the kitchen and bath, written 2025-09-08 before any drawings or bids. Superseded by the plan set on 2026-01-30 in the [[renovation-budget-tracker]]. Kept because the gap between guess and reality is instructive.

| Line | v1 guess |
|---|---|
| Kitchen, contractor | $40,000 |
| Hall bathroom, tub and tile | $14,000 |
| Appliances | $5,500 |
| Other | $3,000 |
| **Total** | **$62,500** |

Funding: renovation savings only, no HELOC.

## Why It Was Retired

The bids came in $9,000 to $16,000 above the contractor lines, the curbless shower added $4,200, and v1 had no contingency at all. The January plan raised the total to $80,000 and added the $18,000 HELOC draw. Even that proved short ([[renovation-contingency]]).
