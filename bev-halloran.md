# Bev Halloran

Next-door neighbor, retired teacher. Keeps our spare key and is the local contact on the alarm list ([[spare-key-and-alarm-contacts]]).

When we travel she takes in mail and packages, waters the garden and checks the basement after heavy rain. She did this for Saltmere Bay in 2025 and for [[halvane-peaks-trip-2026]]. We bring her something from the trip and take care of her cat when she visits her son.

She was patient through the renovation: dumpster in the driveway for four months, a week of tile saws. We gave her notice of each noisy phase and the dates of the panel shutoff, since the utility crew blocked part of her side of the street on 2026-08-27.

Keep: one email before any trip with dates, our contact numbers and who to call for what; the template is in [[close-up-the-house-before-a-trip]].
