# Health Plan Decision 2025-11-10

Renata and I chose Brackwater's high-deductible plan with a health savings account for 2026, replacing the copay plan we had been on since 2019. Kitchen table, Monday evening, about an hour with the comparison sheet open.

## At a Glance

> [!note]
> Decision: high-deductible plan plus HSA for 2026. Premiums $3,960 a year lower, $1,000 employer seed into the HSA, family deductible $4,000, family out-of-pocket maximum $8,000. Accepted the risk of a bad year because the emergency fund stood at $26,000. Dental and vision unchanged.

## Options

| | Copay plan (2025) | High-deductible plan + HSA |
|---|---|---|
| Our premium share | $640 a month, $7,680 a year | $310 a month, $3,720 a year |
| Employer HSA seed | none | $1,000 |
| Family deductible | $1,000 | $4,000 (covers everything except preventive care) |
| Family out-of-pocket maximum | $6,000 | $8,000 |
| Office or therapy visit | $40 copay after nothing | full negotiated price until the deductible is met |

The plans share the same network through Tessaline Health, so [[lionel-grant]] and every provider we use stayed in network. That removed the usual reason not to switch.

## What we compared

Three scenarios from [[health-plan-comparison-2026]], using our 2024 and 2025 claims as a guide:

- Low year (preventive care and a couple of visits, about $800 of billed care): HDHP better by about $4,500.
- Middle year (about $3,000 of care): HDHP better by about $3,000.
- Worst year (out-of-pocket maximum reached): HDHP still better by about $3,000, but it can take $8,000 of cash in one year instead of $6,000.

There is no [[health-plan-break-even]] point between these plans: the premium gap and the seed outweigh the difference in maximums. The risk is cash timing, not total cost. Our 2024 and 2025 claims were both under $1,500.

## Who said what

- I argued for the HDHP on expected cost: two good years of claims, the seed money, and the HSA's tax treatment ([[health-savings-account]]).
- Renata agreed on the numbers but wanted the emergency fund to stay untouched as the backstop for a high year. We wrote that down as the condition.
- Neither of us modeled what would happen if a high year coincided with a renovation overrun. In hindsight that was the gap.

## Decisions and follow-ups

- Renata submitted the election on 2025-11-12.
- HSA payroll contribution set at $300 a month from February 2026 (January was taken by the seed and the new premium).
- Keep all receipts for eligible spending in Paperless-ngx so the HSA can reimburse later if we pay out of pocket ([[should-we-save-hsa-receipts-for-later-reimbursement]]).
- Dental and vision: no change.

## What happened next

Eli's sports injury in 2026-04 and 14 physical therapy visits used the whole deductible by 2026-06 ([[deductible-met-2026-06-12]]). The HSA paid $1,600 and $2,400 came from savings. The decision was partly reversed on [[open-enrollment-review-2026-10-21]]. The stance behind it is archived as [[hdhp-is-the-default-for-a-healthy-family]].
