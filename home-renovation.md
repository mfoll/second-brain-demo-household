## What we have learned so far

Open the floor before you sign the kitchen price. Both change orders came from things nobody could see: rot under the dishwasher and a 100-amp panel that could not carry an induction range plus the heat pump water heater. Supplier dates are a guess until the truck is booked; the cabinet slip cost ten weeks, not money.
