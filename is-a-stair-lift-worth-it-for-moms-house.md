# Is A Stair Lift Worth It For Mom's House?

Open.

## Current Answer

Only if Mom stays at least two more years and wants it. At $4,800 plus $190 a year from year three, it is cheaper than one month of assisted living, but resale value is close to zero and it narrows a hall Mom uses daily.

## For

- Removes the one risk Denise could not fix cheaply ([[denise-farrow-home-assessment-2025-03-19]]).
- Laundry and night trips get easier.

## Against

- Mom is "not keen". A lift she does not use is worth nothing.
- Top landing is tight for the swivel seat ([[stair-lift-quote-2026-09]]).
- If she moves within a year, most of the money is lost ($350 removal).
