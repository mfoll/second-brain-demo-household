# Neighborhood Contractor Recommendations

Summary of replies to my question on the Linden Lane neighbors' email list, 2025-09-22 to 2025-09-29: who did your kitchen or bathroom and would you hire them again?

- Eleven replies, seven named firms. Only firms named by two or more households went on the bid list.
- Quillan Remodeling: three households, all would hire again. One mentioned he "calls you before he spends your money", which turned out true.
- Stanton & Rowe Builders: two households, good work, higher price.
- Birchline Home Builders: one positive, one who said the allowances "ate" their budget. We invited them anyway to have a low bid for comparison.
- Two warnings about a fourth firm leaving jobs half-done; not invited.

Names of the neighbors who replied are not recorded here. [[bev-halloran]] added her own view in person: she had used Quillan for her porch.
