# Distracted driving

## Summary

Encyclopedia article on driving while doing something else that pulls attention from the road, with phones as the main modern example. It covers types of distraction (visual, manual, cognitive) and the safety consequences for drivers, passengers and others.

## Why It Matters

Renata found it the night before we wrote the [[house-driving-agreement]]. The phone rule in the agreement (glovebox or driving mode, navigation set before leaving) came from this reading and from Maya's own suggestion ([[maya-driving-agreement-2026-03-22]]).

## Key Details

- Distraction is grouped into eyes off the road, hands off the wheel and mind off driving; phone use involves all three.
- Hands-free use is still a cognitive distraction.

## Caveats

General background, not specific to new drivers or to our area's law. We did not use any statistic from it in the agreement.
