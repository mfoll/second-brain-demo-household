# Early decision

## Summary

Describes early decision as a binding early application plan: the applicant names the college as first choice and, if admitted with acceptable aid, must enroll and withdraw other applications. The article notes it gives a better statistical chance of admission.

## Why It Matters

Read the day after the [[financial-aid-night-2026-10-08]], before the family meeting. It made the binding clause concrete for Maya and was the main input to our "no" on [[should-maya-apply-early-decision]] ([[application-strategy-meeting-2026-10-11]]).

## Key Details

- Binding commitment, one school.
- The aid-acceptability condition is the only usual way out.
- Odds of admission are higher in the early decision round.

## Caveats

The article describes one national system; our colleges' rules are the ones on their own application pages, which we checked for Kestrel Bay. It does not say how much of the admission advantage is due to stronger applicants applying early.
