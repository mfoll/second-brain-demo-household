# Kitchen Layout Option B

The island layout from Birchwell revision 2 (2025-10-30). Rejected 2025-11-10 in favor of the galley with a peninsula ([[kitchen-design-drawings-birchwell]]).

- 6-foot island with seating for four, range on the outside wall.
- Aisle between range and island: 34 inches, measured by [[dana-whitlock]] on site. Two people cannot pass while the oven is open; the guideline she uses is 42 inches minimum for a work aisle.
- Would have needed a floor outlet in the island, adding about $600.

Renata liked the island. It lost on the aisle. Retired; kept so we do not reargue it.
