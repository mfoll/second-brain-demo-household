# Scholarship

## Summary

Defines a scholarship as financial aid for further education, usually awarded against criteria such as academic merit, athletic skill, financial need, diversity or specific experience.

## Why It Matters

Read after the [[financial-aid-night-2026-10-08]], where the school said local scholarships open in January. Maya will apply to the ones that fit; the amounts would reduce the [[net-price]] we compare in the spring ([[how-will-we-pay-for-mayas-first-year]]).

## Key Details

- Main criteria types: merit, need, athletic, diversity, and field or experience specific.
- Scholarships are gift aid; they are not repaid.

## Caveats

Says nothing about how colleges treat outside scholarships in their own aid packages; some reduce their grants in response. We need to ask each college that question in the spring.
