# Home insurance

## Summary

English Wikipedia article on homeowners insurance: a property policy for a private home that bundles several protections, typically the building, its contents, extra costs when the home cannot be lived in, and liability for accidents at the home.

## Why It Matters

Read before the 2026 renewal call with [[ansel-duarte]], because the kitchen and bath renovation raises the cost to rebuild and I wanted to go in knowing which part of the policy that affects. It is the dwelling part, and the loss-of-use part matters too: during the kitchen remodel we were partly living around a temporary kitchen. Outcome in [[home-insurance-renewal-2026-06-01]].
