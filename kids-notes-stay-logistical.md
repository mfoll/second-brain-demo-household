# Kids' Notes Stay Logistical

## Stance

> [!info]
> Notes about Maya and Eli record schedules, deadlines, fees, logistics and the decisions Renata and I made. Nothing about grades beyond pass or improve, feelings, friendships, discipline, private conversations or health.

## Scope

All of [[support-the-kids]], plus any mention of the kids elsewhere in the vault: the car decision, the calendar, insurance, trips. It does not limit what we talk about as a family; it limits what gets written into a durable, searchable, version-controlled record.

## Why I Think This

- The vault is permanent and shared. Git keeps every version. Maya has read access since 2026-09 and Eli will at 17. Anything about them should be something they would be fine reading years later.
- What I need to resume a task is the logistics: when is the deadline, who paid, who drives. The rest is not needed to act.
- Their records already live somewhere better: grades in the school portal, health in each person's patient portal ([[family-health-records-index]]), essays in Maya's own account. Same principle as [[sensitive-records-stay-at-source]].
- An agent working in the vault should not be able to summarize a teenager's private life, even by accident.

## What Would Change My Mind

- A need for a formal record of a school issue (an accommodation, a dispute) would go into Paperless-ngx with a pointer note, not into prose.
- If either kid asks for something to be recorded about them, it is their call.

## Implications

- Outcomes are written as pass or improve ([[eli-tutoring-wrap-up-2026-05-27]]).
- Health appointments are "Appt" on the [[family-calendar]] and nothing more in kid notes.
- College notes hold the list, deadlines and money, not the application content ([[mayas-college-applications]]).
- People notes about teachers and coaches cover their role only.
