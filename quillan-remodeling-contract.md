# Quillan Remodeling Contract

## Summary

Fixed-price contract with Quillan Remodeling for the kitchen and hall bathroom, signed 2026-01-30 by Renata, me and [[hector-quillan]]. $71,400. Original in the fire safe; scan in Paperless-ngx.

## Key Details

- Scope by reference to Birchwell drawings (kitchen rev. 3, bathroom rev. 2) and Quillan's bid of 2025-12-17.
- Schedule: bathroom 2026-03-02 to about 2026-05-22; kitchen from 2026-06-15, about 14 weeks "subject to cabinet delivery".
- Payments: 10% deposit ($7,140, paid 2026-02-02), stage payments at bathroom completion, kitchen rough-in and cabinet install, 10% retention until final inspection and punch list.
- Changes: written change order priced and signed before work; concealed conditions (rot, wiring, structure) are not in the fixed price. Both of ours fall under this: [[subfloor-rot-change-order-2026-07-08]], [[panel-upgrade-change-order-2026-08-12]].
- Warranty: one year workmanship from final inspection.
- Delays caused by owner-supplied items or suppliers are not compensated either way.

## Caveats

The supplier-delay clause meant the cabinet slip cost us time but not money, and gave us no claim either.
