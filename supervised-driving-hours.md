# Supervised Driving Hours

The practice hours a learner must drive with a licensed adult before the road test. Here: 50 hours, at least 10 at night, with a supervisor aged 21 or over, recorded on a log the parent signs. It is the middle stage of [[graduated-driver-licensing]]: permit, supervised practice, provisional license, full license.

## How we count

- Clock time with the engine running and the learner driving; time parked does not count.
- Night means after sunset; in winter that made the 10 night hours easy, in summer nearly impossible.
- Each entry: date, minutes, day or night, road type (residential, arterial, highway), weather, supervisor initial. Our columns are in [[supervised-driving-log]].

## What the number hides

Fifty hours is a floor. The useful measure was variety: Maya had 31 hours by 2025-11 but almost no rain, highway merging or parking garages. We spent the last 20 hours deliberately on those ([[teen-driving-lessons-learned]]).

## Where it applies

Maya finished at 52 hours (12 night) on 2026-03-08 ([[maya-drivers-license]]). Eli starts in 2027 ([[when-should-eli-start-drivers-ed]]).
