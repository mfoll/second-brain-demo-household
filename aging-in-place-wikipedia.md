# Aging in place

## Summary

Short article built around one definition, attributed to a public health agency: being able to live in your own home and community safely, independently and comfortably, whatever your age, income or ability.

## Why It Matters

Nathan read it two days after [[denise-farrow]]'s home assessment, looking for a word for what Mom kept saying she wanted. The definition's four conditions (home, safely, independently, comfortably) became the frame for plan v1 ([[moms-care-plan-v1-2025-03]]) and, later, for our own [[aging-in-place]] concept note.

## Caveats

Very short and anchored in one country's public health framing. Useful for the definition, not for practical guidance.
