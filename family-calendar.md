# Family Calendar

The shared family calendar: one calendar account owned by Renata with five sub-calendars, on everyone's phone. It is the single place where "who is where, and who is driving" is answered. This note records how we use it; the calendar itself is the record of events.

## Summary

Set up by Renata in 2025-01 during the [[household-budget-reset]], when two missed pickups in one week made the case. Everyone writes to their own sub-calendar; Renata and I can write to all of them. Captured here on 2025-01-12, revised 2026-03-22 when Maya started driving and 2026-08-03 when the Greywillow aide days were added.

## Key Details

Conventions:

- Every event that needs a ride has the driver's initial in square brackets at the start: "[R] Swim meet, away".
- School deadlines go on the kid's calendar with a seven-day reminder ([[mayas-college-applications]]).
- The House calendar holds contractor days, inspections and service dates, for example the final kitchen inspection on 2026-11-05 ([[kitchen-remodel]]).
- The Mom calendar shows aide mornings (Monday and Thursday 9:00 to 13:00 since 2026-08-03, [[home-care-aide-trial]]), Wednesday choir rides and appointments as times only.
- First Sunday of each month: money meeting, 19:30 ([[household-budget-spreadsheet]]).
- Sunday at 20:00 we look at the coming week together; anything not on the calendar by then has no driver.

What never goes on it:

- The reason for any health appointment. Medical events are "Appt" plus the person's name and place, nothing else. Eli's physical therapy visits in 2026-04 to 2026-06 appear only as 14 "Appt - Eli" entries. Rule in [[sensitive-records-stay-at-source]].
- Grades, test scores or college decisions.
- Addresses of people outside the family beyond what the driver needs, and no door codes or alarm codes (those are in Bitwarden).
- Money amounts.

## Caveats

- Claire and Sunita see only the sub-calendars shared with them; the account sharing was checked on 2026-08-03.
- Calendar history is not an archive; anything we need later goes into a note (an Event, a Source) at the time.
- Maya and Eli's school calendars are subscribed read-only ([[band-calendar-2026-27]], [[pellbrook-high-senior-calendar-2026-27]]) and sometimes lag the teachers' emails by a few days.
