# Informal Care Network

The unpaid people around Mom who make living alone work: family, neighbors and friends, each doing one or two things reliably. For Mom: Nathan (Tuesday evenings, Sunday lunch, admin), Renata (midweek call), Maya and Eli (visits), [[fern-ochoa]] (daily text, spare key), [[lorraine-whitby]] (choir and church drives), Claire (calls, visits every six weeks).

Two lessons:

- It is strong but fragile. Each person covers a slot; when one is away, the slot is empty. The [[moms-weekly-support-schedule]] exists so we notice the gaps before Mom does.
- It cannot be asked to grow. Fern and Lorraine help because they are friends; turning that into duties would end it. When more was needed, we paid for it ([[try-paid-help-before-any-move]]).

[[denise-farrow]] called Mom's network "good but thin on weekdays" in 2025, which was right.
