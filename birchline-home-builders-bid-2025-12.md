# Birchline Home Builders Bid 2025-12

## Summary

Bid from Birchline Home Builders, received 2025-12-22, three days late: $64,500 including the shower option. The cheapest number and the weakest bid.

## Key Details

- $9,000 of the price was allowances (tile, fixtures, countertops, lighting), against $2,400 in Quillan's. Pricing our actual choices into those allowances took it to about $72,800.
- Permit "by owner".
- No tile subcontractor named; earliest start 2026-05.
- Two of three references were for new-build work, not remodels.

## Why It Matters

The example behind [[pick-the-contractor-not-the-lowest-bid]]. Rejected at [[renovation-bid-comparison-2026-01-12]].
