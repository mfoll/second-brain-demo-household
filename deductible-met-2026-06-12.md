# Deductible Met 2026-06-12

The family's $4,000 deductible on the 2026 high-deductible plan was fully used with the claim for Eli's 14th physical therapy visit, two months after his sports injury in April.

## Where it went

Totals only, from [[physical-therapy-claims-summary-2026]]: initial visit and imaging $1,140, follow-ups $620, physical therapy $2,240.

## How we paid

- $1,600 from the HSA, leaving $900 in it so the account could keep building.
- $2,400 from savings, which the emergency fund absorbed ([[emergency-fund-low-point-2026-08-31]]).

## What we noticed

- The bills arrived in a cluster, mostly in May. On the copay plan the same care would have cost about $1,000 out of pocket spread over two months.
- Nothing about the plan failed; it did exactly what its design says. The surprise was how fast $4,000 goes in one quarter.
- This is the event that started the rethink in [[open-enrollment-2027]].

Receipts kept for possible later HSA reimbursement ([[should-we-save-hsa-receipts-for-later-reimbursement]]).
