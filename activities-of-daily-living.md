# Activities of daily living

## Summary

Explains activities of daily living as the basic self-care tasks health professionals use to judge how independently someone functions, a concept dating from the 1950s, and notes that later indexes added mobility and related measures.

## Why It Matters

[[denise-farrow]] used the term in her report; Nathan looked it up. It helps separate what Mom does fully on her own (everything in the basic list) from the household tasks that are harder for her (heavy shopping, laundry up and down stairs). The aide covers the second group only ([[home-care-aide-trial]]).

## Caveats

We use the vocabulary, not any scoring. No assessment results are recorded in the vault.
