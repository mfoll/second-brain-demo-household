# Budget Spreadsheet 2024 Envelopes

Archived 2025-04-30. Replaced by the [[household-budget-spreadsheet]] in the [[household-budget-reset]].

## Summary

The envelope-style sheet we used from 2019 to 2024: eight spending categories, each with a monthly cap, tracked loosely from card statements. No lines for annual or irregular costs.

## Key Details

- Categories: groceries, dining, fuel, kids, household, personal (two), gifts.
- No savings rows; savings were "whatever was left", moved by hand at the end of the month.
- In 2024 we ran over the grocery envelope in 9 of 12 months and ended three months with less cash than we started.
- Insurance renewals, car repairs and the property tax were paid from checking with no line at all, which is why every one of them felt like a surprise.

## Why It Was Replaced

It tracked spending but did not plan it, and it ignored the irregular costs that caused most of our cash problems. The fix was a full monthly allocation with [[sinking-fund]] lines, not more envelopes.
