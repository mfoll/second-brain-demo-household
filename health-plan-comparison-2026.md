# Health Plan Comparison 2026

## Summary

My spreadsheet comparing the two Brackwater medical plans for 2026 across three spending scenarios, built 2025-11-08 from [[brackwater-benefits-guide-2026]] and our 2024 and 2025 claim totals.

## Key Details

Total annual cost = our premium share + what we pay for care − the HSA seed.

| Scenario (billed care a year) | Copay plan | HDHP + HSA | Difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low, about $800 | $7,980 | $3,520 | HDHP saves about $4,500 |
| Middle, about $3,000 | $8,680 | $5,720 | HDHP saves about $3,000 |
| High, reaching the out-of-pocket maximum | $13,680 | $10,720 | HDHP saves about $3,000 |

I expected a crossover point and there is none: the premium gap plus the seed ($4,960) is larger than the difference between the two out-of-pocket maximums ($2,000), so the HDHP is cheaper in every scenario. What it changes is how much cash a bad year demands, up to $8,000 instead of $6,000, and how early in the year. See [[health-plan-break-even]]. Our 2024 and 2025 claim totals were both under $1,500.

## Caveats

- Uses billed amounts from past statements, which do not predict injuries.
- Ignores the tax benefit of HSA contributions, which [[imogen-park]] put at a few hundred dollars for us.
