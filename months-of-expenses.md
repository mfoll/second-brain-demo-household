# Months Of Expenses

The unit we size the emergency fund in: how many months the fund could cover our core expenses with no income.

## Core, not total

Core expenses are what must be paid in a bad month: mortgage, HELOC payment, utilities, groceries, insurance, our share of Mom's aide, transport, phones, a minimum for the kids, and the car, home and medical sinking funds. They exclude retirement extras, dining, travel and giving, which we would stop. For us in 2026 that is about $7,000 a month, against about $10,900 of total monthly spending.

Sizing on total spending would have meant a $44,000 target for four months; sizing on core gives $28,000. The difference is the cushion we would cut ourselves.

## Our numbers over time

- 2025: about $6,400 a month core, fund $26,000, about four months.
- 2026-08 low point: $17,360, about two and a half months.
- Target 2027-06: $28,000, four months.
