# Half marathon

## Summary

Describes the half marathon as a road race of 21.0975 km (13.1 miles), often run alongside a marathon or a 5K on a shared course, and also called a 21K.

## Why It Matters

Looked up the day before I set the goal for [[half-marathon-2026]], to get the exact distance for the pace table: 2:05 over 13.1 miles is 9:32 per mile.

## Key Details

- Distance 21.0975 km, 13.1 miles.
- Often held with a full marathon using most of the same course.
