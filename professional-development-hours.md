# Professional Development Hours

The unit the engineering licensing board uses for continuing education: one professional development hour (PDH) per contact hour of qualifying learning. A licensed engineer must log a set number per renewal cycle; mine is 30 hours per two years, including 2 hours of ethics.

## What counts

- Courses, seminars, webinars and conference technical sessions: 1 PDH per hour of instruction.
- College courses: 15 PDH per semester credit.
- Teaching a course for the first time, or publishing a technical paper: counted at fixed rates, capped per cycle.
- Does not count: normal project work, internal project meetings, sales presentations, general management training unless it is on professional practice.

## Records

The engineer keeps the evidence, not the employer. The board audits a sample of renewals and asks for certificates for every hour claimed. My log is [[pdh-log-2026]]; every certificate is a Paperless-ngx document with the "PDH 2025-26" tag ([[household-document-archive]]).

## Habits that work

- Log the day the certificate arrives.
- Aim for 4 hours a quarter; the 2025-26 cycle shows what happens otherwise ([[pe-license-renewal]]).
