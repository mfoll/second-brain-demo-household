# Household Paperwork Retention Rules

How long we keep what, agreed 2025-03 during the budget reset after clearing four boxes of paper from the basement. Applies to Paperless-ngx scans; paper is shredded after scanning unless it is an original kept in the fire safe.

| Kind | Keep | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Tax returns and their supporting documents | 7 years after filing | [[imogen-park]]'s advice; covers any review period comfortably |
| Home improvement invoices, permits, contracts | As long as we own the house, plus 7 years | Cost basis when we sell, and warranty claims |
| Insurance policies | Current plus the previous 3 years; claims files 7 years | Disputes surface late |
| Bank and credit union statements | 3 years, longer if tagged `tax-YYYY` | |
| Medical bills and EOB statements | 7 years; HSA-eligible receipts until reimbursed plus 7 | [[should-we-save-hsa-receipts-for-later-reimbursement]] |
| Pay stubs | Until the year-end wage statement matches | |
| Vehicle records | Life of the car plus 1 year | Resale history; see [[home-and-vehicle-register]] |
| Wills, powers of attorney, deeds, titles, birth certificates | Originals forever, in the fire safe | |
| School notices | End of the school year, except anything needed for applications | |

Review once a year in December: Paperless saved views list documents past their date, and we delete in one sitting. Deletion from Paperless is final once the trash is emptied, so Renata and I both look at the list first.
