# Denise Farrow Home Assessment 2025-03-19

[[denise-farrow]], independent geriatric care manager, assessed Mom's house and support on Wednesday 2025-03-19, 10:00 to 13:00. Present: Mom, and Nathan for the last hour. Written report followed on 2025-04-02 ([[denise-farrow-home-assessment-report-2025-03]]).

## At a Glance

> [!warning]
> The finding that still drives the housing question: Mom's only bedroom and only full bathroom are upstairs, up fourteen stairs with one rail. Everything else Denise listed could be fixed cheaply; that could not.

## What happened

- Denise talked with Mom alone for the first two hours, walked the house with her, and watched her do the stairs, the kitchen and the garden steps.
- In the last hour with Nathan, she went through her provisional list: rugs, lighting, bars, a second rail, a pendant, and "a plan for the day the stairs are too much".
- She was clear that Mom is managing well and that this was about the next five years, not next month.
- She suggested we not look at facilities yet, and that any paid help be household help first.

## Decisions

- Nathan to do the quick fixes within a month (done by 2025-04-26, see [[home-assessment-follow-up-checklist-2025-04]]).
- Trade work in the autumn, after Mom's garden season ([[moms-home-safety-upgrades]]).
- Legal documents next ([[moms-legal-documents]]).
