# Collision avoidance system

## Summary

Article on collision avoidance systems (pre-crash, forward collision warning, collision mitigation): driver-assist systems that try to prevent a crash or reduce its severity. They measure the car's speed, the speed of the vehicle ahead and the gap, warn the driver, and in many systems brake automatically. Radar is the usual sensor, with cameras or lidar added; some systems detect pedestrians.

## Why It Matters

Read after the transmission quote, when I checked what the 2014 Tessera lacks. It has none of this; the 2021 Arden has forward collision warning with automatic braking. This is the center of my side of [[should-maya-drive-the-old-minivan]].

## Key Details

- "Automatic emergency braking" is one function within this family, not a separate technology.
