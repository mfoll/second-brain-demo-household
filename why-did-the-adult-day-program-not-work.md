# Why Did The Adult Day Program Not Work?

Answered 2026-07-03.

## Current Answer

Mom does not want to be in a group setting for a whole day, and the program was chosen more for our peace of mind than for what she asked for. Attendance fell from 6 of 9 days in March to none in June ([[adult-day-attendance-record-2026]]).

## Contributing reasons

- She asked for help with the house, not for activities ([[margarets-stated-preferences-2025-07]]).
- Fixed days clashed with her garden in spring.
- She felt younger and more independent than most participants.
- No one-to-one relationship; the aide model has one ([[home-care-aide-trial]]).
