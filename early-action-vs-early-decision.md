# Early Action Vs Early Decision

Two ways to apply before the regular deadline, with very different commitments.

- **Early action**: apply by an early deadline (usually 2026-11-01 or 2026-11-15 this cycle), hear in mid-December, and stay free to compare every offer until the common commitment date in May. Non-binding.
- **Early decision**: same timing, but binding. If admitted with an aid offer the family considers adequate, the student must enroll and withdraw all other applications. One school only.
- **Restrictive early action**: non-binding but limits other early applications. None of Maya's schools use it.

## Why it matters for us

The difference is not about admissions odds, which are somewhat better under early decision ([[early-decision]]), but about whether we can compare aid offers. For a family that needs to compare [[net-price]], early decision gives that up. That reasoning made our call on [[should-maya-apply-early-decision]] and is the stance in [[compare-aid-offers-before-committing]].

## Words to watch

"Early admission" in general reading covers both ([[early-admission]]); colleges' own pages sometimes say "early" without saying which. Always check the binding clause on the application itself.
