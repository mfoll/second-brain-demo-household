# Household Budget Spreadsheet

The shared zero-based monthly budget Renata and I have run since February 2025. One tab per month, a categories tab, a sinking-funds tab and an emergency-fund ledger tab. Built in the [[household-budget-reset]]; replaced [[budget-spreadsheet-2024-envelopes]].

## Summary

Each month starts with expected take-home pay and assigns every dollar to a category until the "left to assign" cell reads zero. Actuals are entered from the credit union export at the [[monthly-money-meeting]]. Irregular costs are smoothed with [[sinking-fund]] lines. The emergency fund is a separate account tracked on its own tab ([[emergency-fund-ledger]]).
