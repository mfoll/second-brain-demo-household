# Universal design

## Summary

Article on universal design: designing buildings, products and environments that people of any age or ability can use without adaptation. It grew out of the disability rights movement; the familiar example is the curb cut, built for wheelchairs and used by everyone with a stroller or a suitcase.

## Why It Matters

I read this the week before the [[bathroom-design-meeting-2025-11-18]] and brought the curb cut example to the table: a step-free shower helps Mom now, and helps anyone with a broken ankle, a toddler or a bad back. It shaped [[build-for-the-next-twenty-years]] and the [[hall-bathroom-remodel]].

## Key Details

- The approach aims to make a design usable by as many people as possible from the start, rather than retrofitting.
- Related to, but broader than, [[aging-in-place]], which is about one household staying put.
