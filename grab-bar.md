# Grab bar

## Summary

Describes grab bars as safety devices that help a person keep their balance, take some of their weight while moving, or catch themselves in a slip. Notes that caregivers use them when helping someone transfer, and that they appear outside bathrooms too.

## Why It Matters

Read while collecting [[home-safety-quotes-2025-10]] for Mom's house. It made the point that a bar is only as good as what it is fixed to, which is why the installer had to find studs or use anchors rated for the load, and why we put solid blocking behind every wall of our own hall bathroom ([[wall-blocking]]) so bars can go anywhere later.

## Caveats

General; says nothing about load ratings or placement heights, which we took from the installer.
