# Will and testament

## Summary

English Wikipedia article on wills: the legal document in which a person (the testator) says how their estate should be distributed after death and names an executor to manage it until then. Property not covered by a will passes under intestacy rules instead.

## Why It Matters

Background reading before the [[will-consultation-2025-06-24]]. It gave me the vocabulary for Harold's questions (executor, residuary estate) and a reminder that anything outside the will, such as accounts with a named beneficiary, follows its own designation. That is why the [[beneficiary-designations-checklist]] is part of the project.

## Caveats

Formalities differ by jurisdiction. Harold is the authority; the article is orientation only.
