# Wrenfield Driving School Course Outline

The two-page course outline the school emailed when Maya's modules opened on 2025-06-02.

## Summary

- Classroom modules (30 hours): traffic law, signs and signals, sharing the road, hazard perception, impaired and distracted driving, weather, what to do after a crash.
- In-car lessons (6 hours): 1 basic control in a lot; 2 residential; 3 arterial roads and left turns; 4 highway entry and exit; 5 parking, including parallel and garage; 6 mock road test.
- Each lesson ends with a "practice before next time" list for the parent.

## Why It Matters

We used the practice lists to plan the supervised hours between lessons ([[supervised-driving-log]]). The gap it leaves (night and bad weather) is why we planned those deliberately later ([[teen-driving-lessons-learned]]).
