# Renovation Design And Bids

Design the kitchen and hall bathroom with Birchwell Cabinetry, get three bids and sign one contract. Closed on 2026-01-30.

## Outcome

Three bids compared and a contract signed with Quillan Remodeling for $71,400 on 2026-01-30.

## Current State

- Done. Contract signed with [[hector-quillan]]; construction moved to [[hall-bathroom-remodel]] and [[kitchen-remodel]].

## Working Context

[[dana-whitlock]] at Birchwell drew the kitchen in three rounds between 2025-09 and 2025-11. Layout B (island with seating) lost to the galley-plus-peninsula layout because the island left 34 inches of aisle at the range; the rejected drawing is kept as [[kitchen-layout-option-b]]. The bathroom was settled at the [[bathroom-design-meeting-2025-11-18]].

The same drawing set went to three builders on 2025-11-24, with bids due 2025-12-19:

| Bidder | Price | Start | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quillan Remodeling | $71,400 (incl. $4,200 shower) | 2026-03-02 | Own tile sub, fixed price, 10% retention |
| Stanton & Rowe Builders | $78,900 | 2026-02-16 | Fixed price, larger crew |
| Birchline Home Builders | $64,500 | 2026-05 | $9,000 in allowances, no tile sub named |

Compared on 2026-01-12 ([[renovation-bid-comparison-2026-01-12]]). Birchline was cheapest on paper but its allowances would have pushed it past Quillan once we picked real fixtures. Stanton & Rowe was solid and $7,500 more. We picked Quillan for price, references and a named tile setter ([[sione-tupou]]).
