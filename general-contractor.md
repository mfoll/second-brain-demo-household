# General contractor

## Summary

Article on the general contractor, or builder: the person or firm that oversees a construction site day to day, manages the trades and suppliers, and keeps everyone informed. It also covers how contractors price work and carry overhead.

## Why It Matters

Background when we started asking for bids in 2025-10. It made clear what we were paying Quillan for beyond labor: coordination of [[sione-tupou]], [[lena-marsh]], the plumber and the inspections. That framing is behind [[pick-the-contractor-not-the-lowest-bid]].

## Key Details

- The general contractor carries responsibility for the subcontractors' work, which is why we wanted the tile setter named in the bid.
- Markup on subcontracted work is normal and pays for that coordination.
