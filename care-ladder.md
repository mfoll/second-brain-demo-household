# Care Ladder

Our name for the steps of support between living fully independently and full-time care, ordered roughly by cost and by how much of a person's life changes. Useful because family arguments about care are often arguments about which rung, with nobody saying so.

1. **Family and neighbors.** Visits, calls, a daily text ([[informal-care-network]]).
2. **House changes.** Bars, rails, lighting, a pendant ([[moms-home-safety-upgrades]]).
3. **Paid help at home, a few hours a week.** Household tasks and company ([[home-care-aide-trial]]).
4. **Day programs.** Company and meals outside the home ([[adult-day-program-trial]]).
5. **More paid hours, up to daily visits.**
6. **A smaller, step-free home**, such as a senior apartment ([[senior-apartment-listings-2026-10]]).
7. **[[assisted-living|Assisted living]].**
8. Nursing care. Not on the table for Mom and not discussed here.

Rungs 3 and 4 are side by side, not ordered: Mom skipped 4 after trying it. Claire's position is essentially "go to 7 soon"; Mom's is "stay at 3 for now". Naming the rung made the [[family-care-call-2026-07-26]] easier.
