# Gather Tax Documents

How I collect the documents for [[imogen-park]] each year, by the end of February. Written for the 2025 return from [[tax-document-request-email-2026-01]]; updated for the 2026 return.

## Steps

1. Through the year: tag anything tax-relevant `tax-YYYY` on arrival ([[file-a-paper-document]]).
2. Mid-January: download year-end statements rather than waiting for paper: credit union interest and mortgage interest, retirement contribution confirmations.
3. Late January: both wage statements from the employer portals. From the 2026 return: the HSA administrator's contribution and distribution forms.
4. Collect charitable receipts above the reporting threshold.
5. Credits: for any energy-efficiency purchase, the itemized invoice with labor and the manufacturer's certification statement. Fill in the credit worksheet ([[home-energy-credit-worksheet-2025]] is the template).
6. Write a one-page cover note: what changed from last year.
7. Upload everything to Imogen's client portal by 02-28. Never by email.
8. After filing: save the filed return and acceptance notice to Paperless-ngx and write the year's summary note ([[tax-return-2025-summary]]).

## New for the 2026 return

- HSA forms (two).
- The health plan reimbursements, if we reimburse any 2026 receipts from the HSA during 2027.
- No energy credit expected.

## Verification

Imogen confirms receipt with no missing items by mid-March; refund or balance due goes to the emergency fund decision at the next [[monthly-money-meeting]].
