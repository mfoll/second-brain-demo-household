# Stanton And Rowe Builders Bid 2025-12

## Summary

Fixed-price bid from Stanton & Rowe Builders, received 2025-12-19: $78,900 including the curbless shower option at $4,800. Earliest start 2026-02-16.

## Key Details

- Larger crew, would have run kitchen and bathroom in parallel and finished by about 2026-06.
- Same scope as Quillan, slightly better cabinet installation allowance, tile subcontractor "from our list".
- 15% deposit, the rest monthly.

## Why It Matters

Credible second choice, $7,500 more than Quillan. Running both rooms at once would have left us with no working bathroom upstairs except the primary for four months and no kitchen at the same time, which Renata ruled out. Rejected at [[renovation-bid-comparison-2026-01-12]].
