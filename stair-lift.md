# Stair lift

## Summary

Describes a stair lift as a motorized chair or platform on a rail fixed to the stair treads, which carries a person up and down the stairs.

## Why It Matters

Read before the stair lift quote for Mom's house ([[stair-lift-quote-2026-09]]). Mom's stairs are straight, which keeps the price down, but the article's description of getting on and off at the top made Nathan check the landing width, which turned out to be tight.

## Caveats

Very short; nothing on costs, reliability or resale. Those came from the quote.
