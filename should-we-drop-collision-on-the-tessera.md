# Should We Drop Collision On The Tessera?

Raised 2025-02-09 during the budget reset; answered 2025-02-14.

## Current Answer

Yes. Collision on the 2014 Tessera cost about $310 a year with a $1,000 deductible, on a car then worth about $5,500. The most it could ever pay was $4,500. We kept liability and comprehensive.

## Reasoning

- Rule of thumb we used: drop collision when the annual premium exceeds about 10% of the car's value minus the deductible.
- The savings went into the car sinking fund instead.
