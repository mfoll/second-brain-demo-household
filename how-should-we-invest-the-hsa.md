# How Should We Invest The HSA?

Opened 2026-10-21. The HSA stays open after we leave the high-deductible plan, with $2,180 in it and no new contributions from 2027.

## Current State

Renata moved $1,680 into the administrator's broad index fund option on 2026-10-22 and left $500 in cash, the minimum before investing.

## The real question

Is the HSA a spending account for the next big bill, or a long-term account we leave alone? If long-term, it should be fully invested and we should pay medical costs from the budget's medical sinking fund instead. If spending, cash is fine and investing is a distraction.

## Options

| Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| Leave invested, never touch | Grows tax-free; receipts from 2026 can be reimbursed years later | Small balance; the gain is modest |
| Keep as cash for 2027 costs | Simple; covers copays | Loses the main advantage of the account |
| Reimburse the $2,400 of 2026 receipts now and refill the emergency fund | Speeds the rebuild by about three months | Empties the HSA |

## Inputs missing

- The administrator's fee schedule for the investment option (asked 2026-10-22).
- Whether we expect to return to an HDHP in 2028; if yes, a long-term view makes more sense.
