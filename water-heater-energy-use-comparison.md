# Water Heater Energy Use Comparison

## Summary

My comparison of twelve months of utility bills before (2024-06 to 2025-05) and after (2025-06 to 2026-05) the heat pump water heater. Gas use fell about 21% and electricity rose about 9%; the net saving is roughly $310 a year at current rates.

## Key Details

- Gas: summer months show the clearest drop, since the furnace is off and the water heater was the only gas load.
- Electricity: +1,050 kWh over the year, about $165.
- Gas saving about $475. Net about $310.
- Weather: the 2025-26 winter was a little colder, which flatters nothing; I did not correct for it.

## Caveats

Two bills only, no submetering. The basement is a bit cooler in summer, which we like. Payback of the extra cost over a gas unit (about $700 after the credit) is roughly two and a half years. Used in [[heat-pump-payback]].
