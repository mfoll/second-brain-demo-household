# Cement board

## Summary

Article on cement board: sheets of cement with reinforcing fibers in several thicknesses, used mainly as a tile backer on walls and floors, and also as a base for exterior stucco.

## Why It Matters

Hector's bid specified cement board on the shower walls under a sheet membrane. I read this to understand why not drywall. Used in [[hall-bathroom-remodel]]; [[sione-tupou]] did the installation and flood test.

## Key Details

- Cement board tolerates water without rotting, but it is not itself waterproof; the membrane does that job.
- It is fixed to studs for walls or over plywood for floors.
