# Kitchen Design Drawings Birchwell

## Summary

Revision 3 of the kitchen drawings from [[dana-whitlock]], 2025-11-28: plan, four elevations and a cabinet schedule. The set that went out to bid and into the contract.

## Key Details

- Galley with a peninsula, seating for three. 42-inch aisle between range and peninsula.
- Range moved 30 inches from the old position to an inside wall, which needed a new 50-amp circuit (one reason the panel became a question).
- Pantry cabinet replaces the old broom closet.
- Rev. 1 (2025-10-02) was a straight copy of the old layout; rev. 2 (2025-10-30) added the island that we dropped ([[kitchen-layout-option-b]]).
