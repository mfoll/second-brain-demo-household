# Panel Upgrade Change Order 2026-08-12

Change order 2, signed 2026-08-12: upgrade the electrical service from 100 to 200 amps with a new panel, $2,900.

## Why

[[lena-marsh]]'s load calculation ([[kitchen-electrical-load-calculation]]) showed the old service at about 96% of capacity with the induction range added to the heat pump water heater, dryer and AC. Options were a $900 load-management device or the upgrade. We chose the upgrade because any future heat pump for heating ([[home-energy-retrofits]]) would need it anyway, and doing it while the walls were open saved a second disruption.

## What It Included

New 200-amp panel ([[distribution-board]]), new meter base, utility disconnect and reconnect on 2026-08-27, permit amendment, labeled circuit schedule.

## Effect

Contract plus changes now $78,150; projected total $86,240, $6,240 over the $80,000 plan ([[renovation-budget-tracker]]). The overrun came from the emergency fund, which with the health deductible gap fell to $17,360 and started the [[emergency-fund-rebuild]].
