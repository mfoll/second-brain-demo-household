# Log A New Appliance Or Warranty

What to do within 30 days of anything new arriving that costs more than $300 or has a warranty: appliances, systems, cars, tools.

## Steps

1. **Scan the receipt and warranty card** into Paperless-ngx with the tag "house" or the car's tag. Note the document ID ([[household-document-archive]]).
2. **Register the warranty** with the maker if registration changes the start date or coverage. Kestova starts from delivery only if registered within 30 days ([[marisol-vega]]). Logins go into Bitwarden under "House".
3. **Add a row to the [[home-and-vehicle-register]]**: item, location, brand and model, serial, purchase date, price, installer, warranty end, [[service-interval]], expected life, manual link, Paperless ID.
4. **Download the manual** as PDF and link it from the register; keep the paper manual in the drawer nearest the item.
5. **Set calendar reminders** for the first service and one month before the warranty ends.
6. **Decline extended warranties** unless the item is outside the scope of [[skip-extended-warranties]].

## Guardrails

- No account numbers, logins or full receipts in the vault; the register holds serials because service lines ask for them.
- If the item replaced something, mark the old row "retired" with the date rather than deleting it.

## Verification

Each October during the [[seasonal-house-maintenance-checklist]], sort the register by warranty end and check nothing expires unnoticed in the next six months.
