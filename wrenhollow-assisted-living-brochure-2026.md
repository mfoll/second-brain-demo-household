# Wrenhollow Assisted Living Brochure 2026

Brochure and 2026 price sheet for Wrenhollow Assisted Living, on the north side of Pellbrook, about 15 minutes from us. Claire collected it on her visit in 2026-06 and mailed us a copy.

## Key Details

- One-bedroom apartment with kitchenette, three meals a day, housekeeping, activities, staff on site 24 hours.
- From $5,400 a month for the base level of support; higher levels cost more after an assessment.
- One-time community fee $2,500. Waiting list for one-bedrooms, about four months in mid-2026.
- Short-term respite stays available at a daily rate.

## Why It Matters

Option C in [[should-mom-stay-in-her-house]], and the place Claire favors ([[claire-email-assisted-living-case-2026-07]]). Mom has not visited and has said she will not.

## Caveats

Marketing material. Prices for 2027 not yet published. Nothing here about what happens when needs exceed assisted living.
