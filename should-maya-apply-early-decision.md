# Should Maya Apply Early Decision?

## Current Answer

> [!info]
> No. Maya applies early action (non-binding) to Kestrel Bay University and Corliss College by 2026-11-01 and regular decision to the other six. Decided at the [[application-strategy-meeting-2026-10-11]].

## Why It Mattered

Early decision is binding: an admitted student with an acceptable aid offer must enroll and withdraw elsewhere ([[early-decision]]). Kestrel Bay offers it and was Maya's first choice after the June visit ([[college-visits-2026-06-22]]). Admission odds are better in the early decision round, which is why Maya raised it.

## What Decided It

- We cannot judge whether an offer is "acceptable" without at least two others to compare ([[net-price]]).
- Our cash position in 2026-27 is weaker than usual: emergency fund $19,235 against a $28,000 target ([[emergency-fund-rebuild]]), and a car decision in December.
- Early action at the same school gets most of the timing benefit without the commitment ([[early-action-vs-early-decision]]).

## What Would Reopen It

Nothing this cycle; the early decision deadline passed on 2026-11-01. For Eli in 2028 the answer depends on our savings then, not on this year's reasoning. Our general stance is in [[compare-aid-offers-before-committing]].
