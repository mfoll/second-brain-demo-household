# Trial With Review Date

A decision pattern we now use for care changes: agree to try something for a fixed period, set the review date and who attends at the time of agreeing, and say in advance what would count as success.

Why it works for us:

- It lets someone who disagrees agree to the trial without conceding the argument. Claire accepted the aide trial on these terms ([[family-care-call-2026-07-26]]).
- It gives Mom a way out that is not a fight.
- It stops drift. The day program ran four months half-dead because there was no review date ([[adult-day-program-lessons]]).

It is the same as a pilot with a gate review at work. The household version needs one extra rule: the person being cared for attends the review. The stance behind it is [[care-decisions-need-a-written-review-date]].
