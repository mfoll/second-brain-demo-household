# Home Insurance Policy

## Summary

Summary of the Merriline Mutual homeowners policy on the Linden Lane house as renewed on 2026-06-01. Policy number and the declarations page are in Bitwarden and Paperless-ngx.

## Key Details

- Dwelling coverage raised at the 2026 renewal to reflect the renovation; [[ansel-duarte]] used the contract value and the finished hall bathroom to re-estimate rebuild cost.
- Contents at the policy's standard share of dwelling; no separate schedule for jewelry or equipment.
- Loss of use included; would cover a rental if the house became unlivable.
- Liability at the level required under the [[umbrella-insurance-policy]].
- All-perils deductible $2,500 (raised from $1,000 in 2025-03 during the [[household-budget-reset]], saving $190 a year). Separate wind and hail deductible of 2% of dwelling coverage.
- Annual premium: $1,860 for 2025-26; $2,140 for 2026-27.

## Caveats

- The 1998 asphalt roof is the next big exposure; Merriline may adjust roof coverage to depreciated value once it passes 30 years. Relevant to [[roof-replacement]].
- Contractor's work during the remodel was covered by Quillan's own liability insurance; certificate saved in Paperless-ngx with the contract.
