# Monthly Money Meeting

How Renata and I run the first-Sunday money meeting. Target 45 minutes. In use since [[first-money-meeting-2025-02-02]].

## Goal

Last month's actuals entered, this month's plan at zero, and any decision over $500 made together rather than discovered later.

## Inputs

- Credit union export for last month (downloaded the morning of, deleted after).
- The [[household-budget-spreadsheet]].
- The [[annual-money-calendar]] row for the coming month.
- Paperless-ngx inbox.

## Steps

1. Import last month's transactions; each of us categorizes our own categories. (15 minutes)
2. Look at three numbers only: left to assign, emergency fund balance ([[emergency-fund-ledger]]), sinking-fund totals.
3. Assign this month's take-home until left-to-assign is zero. Changes from last month get a one-line reason in the notes column.
4. Check the coming month on the annual calendar: renewals, fees, deadlines.
5. Process the Paperless-ngx inbox: tag, title, file ([[file-a-paper-document]]).
6. Decisions: anything over $500 that is coming up. Write the outcome in the right vault note, not just the sheet.
7. Save a PDF snapshot of the month's tab to Paperless-ngx with tag `budget`.

## Guardrails

- No account numbers or full statements leave the credit union app; the sheet holds totals.
- If we disagree on something big, park it as a Question in the vault and come back next month rather than settle it at the table.
- Skipped meetings get rescheduled within the week, not merged into the next month.

## Verification

Left-to-assign is zero, the ledger balance matches the credit union app, and the snapshot is in Paperless-ngx.
