# Gap year

## Summary

Overview of the gap year: a break, typically between high school and college, spent travelling, working, volunteering or studying, and not necessarily a full year.

## Why It Matters

Maya mentioned a gap year once at dinner in 2026-02. I read this to be ready if it came up again; it did not, and her applications went ahead for fall 2027. Kept in [[college-planning]] because Eli might ask in 2028, and because a deferred start would change the timing of [[how-will-we-pay-for-mayas-first-year]].

## Key Details

- Activities: travel, work, volunteering, courses.
- Length can be months to a few years.

## Caveats

Says little about how individual colleges handle deferral requests; that is on each college's admission page and would need checking at the time.
