# Heat pump

## Summary

Article on heat pumps: machines that move heat from one place to another using mechanical work, typically electricity driving a compressor. The same refrigeration cycle that cools one side warms the other. The page "Heat pump water heater" redirects here.

## Why It Matters

First read the day after the gas water heater failed, when [[ray-okonjo]] offered a heat pump unit. Reread in 2026 for the furnace question ([[should-we-replace-the-furnace-with-a-heat-pump]]). Belongs to the [[home-energy-retrofits]] cluster.

## Key Details

- Efficiency is expressed as heat moved per unit of electricity, which is why a heat pump can deliver more heat than the energy it draws.
- Performance drops as the outdoor or source temperature falls; for a water heater in a basement this matters little, for whole-house heating it matters a lot.
