# Umbrella insurance

## Summary

English Wikipedia article on umbrella insurance: liability cover that pays once the limits of underlying policies such as auto and home are exhausted, and that can also act as first-line cover for some losses those policies exclude. It distinguishes this from plain excess insurance, which only extends existing limits.

## Why It Matters

Read two days after Maya was added to the auto policy, when I started wondering whether our $1 million umbrella was enough with a teenage driver in the house ([[should-we-raise-the-umbrella-limit]]). The layering explanation also clarified why Merriline insists on minimum liability limits on the auto and home policies underneath ([[umbrella-insurance-policy]]).

## Caveats

Nothing in the article says what limit a household should carry. That is a judgment for us and [[ansel-duarte]].
