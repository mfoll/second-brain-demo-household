# Graduated driver licensing

## Summary

Encyclopedia overview of graduated licensing: new drivers move through stages (a supervised learner stage, a provisional stage with limits, then a full license) so that experience is gained gradually and the riskiest situations are delayed. Typical provisional limits are on night driving and on carrying young passengers.

## Why It Matters

I read it the weekend before Maya's permit test so we would understand why the rules are shaped the way they are, not just what they are. It is the background for [[supervised-driving-hours]] and for the provisional limits we copied into the [[house-driving-agreement]].

## Key Details

- Three stages: learner, intermediate or provisional, full.
- Restrictions in the middle stage usually cover night-time driving and passengers, and sometimes phone use.
- The article describes how rules differ between jurisdictions; ours come from the licensing office handbook, not from here.
