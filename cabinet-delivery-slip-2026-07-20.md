# Cabinet Delivery Slip 2026-07-20

The date the cabinets should have arrived and did not. Birchwell had given notice on 2026-07-02 that delivery moved to the week of 2026-09-28 ([[birchwell-delay-notice-email]]).

Kept as an Event because it explains the schedule: the kitchen that should have been done in late August is finishing in November, the final inspection moved to 2026-11-05, and we lived on the [[temporary-kitchen-setup]] for 15 weeks instead of 5.

Cost: no money (supplier delays are no-fault under [[quillan-remodeling-contract]]; Birchwell waived the $240 delivery fee). Hector used the gap for the panel upgrade, rough-in and drywall, so the actual loss was about ten weeks.

What I would do differently: ask the cabinet maker for a production slot date in writing, not a delivery estimate.
