# Service Interval

How often something needs routine care: every 7,500 miles, every three months, every October. The register stores the interval; the [[family-calendar]] holds the next date.

Why split them: dates go stale and intervals do not. When a service happens early or late, I move the calendar reminder and leave the register alone.

Ours fall into three kinds:

- Mileage: oil and [[tire-rotation]] on both cars at 7,500 miles ([[joon-ho-bae]]).
- Calendar: furnace each October, AC each April, dryer vent yearly, water heater filter every three months.
- Seasonal checklist items with no fixed interval but a season: gutters, attic, sump pump test ([[seasonal-house-maintenance-checklist]]).

Rule: if the maker's interval and the mechanic's differ, use the shorter one unless it costs more than $100 extra a year, then ask why.
