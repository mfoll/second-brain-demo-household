# Change order

## Summary

Short article on change orders in construction project management. Outside North America the same thing is called a variation or variation order: any change to the work that the contract already agreed counts as one.

## Why It Matters

I read it the evening after Hector priced the subfloor repair ([[subfloor-rot-change-order-2026-07-08]]), mainly to check that the way our contract handles changes is normal. It is: a written, priced change signed by both sides before work starts. Linked from [[kitchen-and-bath-renovation]].

## Key Details

- The term covers additions, deletions and substitutions, not only extra cost.
- Useful vocabulary when reading other people's renovation stories: "variation" means the same thing.
- Our contract's change clause is summarized in [[quillan-remodeling-contract]]; both our change orders followed it.
