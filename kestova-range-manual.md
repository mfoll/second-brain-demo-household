# Kestova Range Manual

Owner's manual for the Kestova 30-inch induction range. Capture of the parts we will need again:

- Warranty: two years parts and labor from the purchase date, or from delivery if registered within 30 days. Glass cooktop covered for defects only, not impact or heat damage.
- Cookware must be magnetic; two old aluminum pans went to the Pellbrook Senior Center rummage sale.
- Child lock: hold the lock key for three seconds.
- Error codes table on page 41; most mean the pan is not detected.
- Needs a dedicated 40 or 50 amp circuit; ours is 50 ([[kitchen-electrical-load-calculation]]).
