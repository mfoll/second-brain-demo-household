# Pay A Professional For The Tax Return

## Stance

While our return has anything beyond wages and standard deductions, such as energy credits, the HSA, or later a college-related credit, the $650 to $700 we pay [[imogen-park]] is worth it.

## Why I Think This

- She caught that the energy credit needed the manufacturer's certification, which I would have missed ([[tax-documents-handoff-2026-02-27]]).
- My time on the return was about four hours of gathering; doing it myself would be another ten, with more risk of an error on the credit.
- She keeps a three-year view of our returns, which helps when a credit or carryover spans years, as the heat pump question might.

## What Would Change My Mind

- Two years in a row with nothing beyond wages and standard items.
- A fee above about $1,000.

## Implications

Keep the document process tight so her time goes on judgment, not chasing statements ([[gather-tax-documents]]).
