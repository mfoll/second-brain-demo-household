# Travel insurance

## Summary

Article on travel insurance: cover against unexpected financial losses while traveling abroad or at home. Basic policies pay emergency medical costs abroad; fuller ones add trip cancellation, lost luggage, delays, personal liability and more.

## Why It Matters

Checked before booking Halvane Peaks. The trip was domestic, our health plan covers us anywhere in the country, and the lodging was refundable to 14 days before arrival, so we skipped it. Recorded in [[halvane-peaks-trip-2026]].

## Key Details

- The cancellation part is what would matter for us, and it only pays if the reason is on the policy's list.
- Worth revisiting for any trip with nonrefundable bookings above about $1,500.
