# Renovation Bid Comparison 2026-01-12

Sunday evening, Renata and me with the three bids printed out. Decision: Quillan Remodeling.

## Normalized Comparison

I priced our actual choices into each bidder's allowances so the numbers compared like with like:

| | Quillan | Stanton & Rowe | Birchline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bid as submitted | $71,400 | $78,900 | $64,500 |
| Allowance shortfall for our choices | $0 | $0 | +$7,200 |
| Permit (if excluded) | incl. | incl. | +$1,100 |
| Comparable total | $71,400 | $78,900 | about $72,800 |
| Start | 2026-03-02 | 2026-02-16 | 2026-05 |
| Tile setter named | Yes | No | No |
| Neighbor references | 3, all positive | 2 | 1 mixed |

Sources: [[quillan-remodeling-bid-2025-12]], [[stanton-and-rowe-bid-2025-12]], [[birchline-home-builders-bid-2025-12]], [[neighborhood-contractor-recommendations]].

## Reasoning

Birchline's low number disappeared once the allowances were real, and the later start would have pushed the kitchen into winter. Stanton & Rowe was good but $7,500 more and wanted to run both rooms at once. Quillan was cheapest on a like-for-like basis, had the best references and named [[sione-tupou]] for the tile, which mattered most for the curbless shower.
