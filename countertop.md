# Countertop

## Summary

Article on countertops (also worktops or benchtops): flat work surfaces in kitchens, bathrooms and workrooms, usually set on cabinets at a working height. It surveys materials, including engineered stone, natural stone, laminate and wood, and their trade-offs in durability and upkeep.

## Why It Matters

Read when Dana sent three countertop options for the kitchen. It was a neutral starting point before the sales sheets. We picked quartz ([[which-countertop-material]], [[countertop-quote-quartz]]).

## Key Details

- Engineered stone is non-porous and needs no sealing; natural granite needs periodic sealing; wood needs oiling and dislikes standing water near a sink.
- Heat tolerance varies; we keep a trivet by the range regardless.
