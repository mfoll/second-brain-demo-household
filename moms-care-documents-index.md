## Why It Matters

This is the answer to "where do we keep Mom's medical information?": in her clinic's patient portal, with Nathan's proxy access, not here. When the pendant service calls at night, or a hospital asks who can decide, the person on the phone needs to know where the proxy is and who is named, quickly. This table gives that in one place without turning the vault into a copy of her records.

It also makes the access visible. Mom asked in 2025 who could see what; this was the answer we showed her.

## Caveats

- The table is only as good as its last check. If a date in the last column is older than a year, check before relying on it.
- Paperless IDs are stable unless a document is re-scanned; if a lookup fails, search Paperless by title.
- Never add a number, login or clinical detail here, even temporarily. Put it in Bitwarden or leave it in the portal.
