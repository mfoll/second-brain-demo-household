# Health Plan Comparison 2027

## Summary

The 2027 version of the comparison, rebuilt 2026-10-19 with the 2027 rates and, this time, a cash-timing column: how much money each plan could demand in a single quarter. Renata added the "fund at risk" row.

## Key Details

| Scenario | Copay plan | HDHP + HSA | Peak quarter cash, HDHP |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low (about $800 of care) | $8,100 | $3,640 | under $500 |
| Middle (about $3,000) | $8,800 | $5,840 | up to $3,000 |
| A 2026-like year | about $8,800 | about $7,220 | up to $4,000 |
| Worst case (out-of-pocket maximum) | $13,800 | $10,840 | up to $8,000 |

Rows include premiums ($650 and $320 a month), the $1,000 seed on the HDHP, and copays or coinsurance as applicable.

Fund at risk: with the emergency fund planned at about $22,000 in early 2027, a 2026-like year on the HDHP takes up to 18% of it in one quarter; on the copay plan, under 5%.

## Why It Matters

The cost rows alone still favor the HDHP. The cash-timing rows are what decided the [[open-enrollment-review-2026-10-21]]. See [[hdhp-needs-a-full-emergency-fund]].

## Caveats

Expected use is not modeled per person and should not be; scenarios are deliberately generic.
