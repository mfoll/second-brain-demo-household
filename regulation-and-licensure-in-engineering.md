# Regulation and licensure in engineering

## Summary

Explains that jurisdictions regulate engineering to protect public safety and welfare, and define how an engineer becomes licensed to offer engineering services to the public. It surveys the different systems.

## Why It Matters

Background for [[pe-license-renewal]]: the reason a stormwater or bridge engineer at Marrowfield has to keep a license current, and why the board, not the employer, audits the hours. Read the same evening as [[professional-development]].

## Key Details

- Licensure exists to protect the public.
- Each jurisdiction sets its own licensing process.

## Caveats

Comparative and general; nothing in it is about our board. Renewal rules come from [[engineering-board-renewal-notice-2026]].
