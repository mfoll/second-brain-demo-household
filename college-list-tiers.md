# College List Tiers

Sorting a college list into three groups by how likely admission is, so the list neither wastes applications nor risks no offers.

- **Likely**: the student's record is clearly above the typical admitted student, and the family could afford it on expected aid. At least two.
- **Match**: the record is about typical for admitted students. The bulk of the list.
- **Reach**: admission is uncertain for anyone, or the record is below typical. One to three.

Our working shape, from [[janelle-forbes]]: two likely, four match, two reach, eight in all ([[how-many-colleges-should-maya-apply-to]]).

## Two cautions

- A "likely" school is only likely if it is also affordable. We added a cost check to each tier using [[net-price]], which moved one school from likely to match for us.
- Tiers come from the school's published profiles and the counselor's experience; they are estimates. Maya's own tier for each school is in [[maya-college-list]], and her record stays out of the vault.
