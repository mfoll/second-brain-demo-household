# Kitchen And Bath Renovation

Umbrella for remodeling the original 1998 kitchen and the hall bathroom with Quillan Remodeling. Design and bids ran through 2025; the bathroom was done in spring 2026; the kitchen is finishing now.

## Outcome

Kitchen and hall bathroom remodeled by Quillan Remodeling, final inspection passed, and the total inside the $80,000 plan or the overrun explained and funded.

## Current State

- [[hall-bathroom-remodel]] done 2026-05-22, inspection passed 2026-05-20.
- [[kitchen-remodel]] in finishing: countertops installed 2026-10-14, backsplash and punch list left, final inspection 2026-11-05.
- Spend is $86,240 against $80,000, so $6,240 over. Two change orders ($3,850 subfloor, $2,900 panel) explain all of it; appliances came in $240 over and temporary-kitchen costs $750 under ([[renovation-budget-tracker]]).
- The overrun was paid from the emergency fund, which started the [[emergency-fund-rebuild]] and paused the [[will-and-guardianship-update]]. See [[how-should-we-fund-the-renovation-overrun]].
- Final payment to Quillan (10%, $7,815 of contract plus changes) is held until the final inspection.

## Scope and Boundaries

Kitchen and hall bathroom only, under one contract signed 2026-01-30 for $71,400 and one building permit. The primary bathroom keeps its tub and is not part of this ([[should-the-primary-bathroom-keep-its-tub]]). Roof and furnace are separate decisions ([[roof-replacement]], [[home-energy-retrofits]]).

## Working Context

Funding: $62,000 of renovation savings plus an $18,000 draw on the credit union home equity line ([[renovation-heloc-draw]]). Kestova appliances are owner-bought, so their warranties are ours to register, not Quillan's.

The two design choices that will matter in twenty years are in the bathroom: curbless shower with a [[linear-drain]] and [[wall-blocking]] for grab bars, decided at the [[bathroom-design-meeting-2025-11-18]]. The reasoning is in [[aging-in-place]] and [[build-for-the-next-twenty-years]].
