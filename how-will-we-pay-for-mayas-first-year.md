# How Will We Pay For Maya's First Year?

## Why It Matters

Maya starts college in 2027-08 or 2027-09. Her first-year bill lands at the same time as the emergency fund rebuild target (2027-06-30, [[emergency-fund-rebuild]]) and right after whatever we decide about the car ([[should-maya-drive-the-old-minivan]]).

## What We Know

- College savings account: $31,800 as of 2026-09-30, contributions $250 a month since 2012. That covers most of one year at the in-county public option and much less elsewhere.
- Net prices from the calculators so far ([[college-cost-comparison]]): Wrenfield State about $16,900 living on campus, Kestrel Bay about $24,500, Harlow about $31,000 before any merit aid. These are estimates, not offers ([[net-price]]).
- Maya's job: she plans to save about $2,500 by summer 2027 toward books and living costs.

## Options

1. Savings first, then cash flow from the budget for the gap (about $600 a month across both of us for a mid-priced school).
2. A national student loan in Maya's name, capped at the standard first-year amount; we do not want more debt than that.
3. A cheaper first year (live at home and attend Wrenfield State) if offers are poor.

No home equity borrowing for tuition: the line already carries the $18,000 renovation draw ([[renovation-heloc-draw]]).

## Missing Inputs

- Actual aid offers, expected 2027-03 to 2027-04.
- The 2027 budget after the health plan change (+$330 a month, [[open-enrollment-review-2026-10-21]]) and the car decision.

## Target

Decide by 2027-04-25, a week before most commitment deadlines on 2027-05-01. Stance: [[compare-aid-offers-before-committing]].
