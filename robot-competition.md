# Robot competition

## Summary

Short article on robot competitions: events where robots are tested against tasks or each other. Many are for schools, others for professionals and hobbyists.

## Why It Matters

Read after the [[robotics-parent-meeting-2026-09-10]] to understand what Eli's season builds towards. It helped me make sense of the build-then-compete structure in the [[robotics-team-handbook-2026]] and why the team asks for Saturdays in January and February.

## Key Details

- Robots are assessed on abilities and characteristics, usually by outperforming other robots.
- School competitions are a large share of the field.
