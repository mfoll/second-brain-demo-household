# Denise Farrow Home Assessment Report 2025-03

Nine-page written report from [[denise-farrow]] after her visit to Mom's house on 2025-03-19, received 2025-04-02. Scan in Paperless-ngx, ID 1102. Paper copy with Claire.

## Summary

Three parts: the house, daily routines, and the support around Mom. Only the house and support parts are summarized here; the routine section talks about Mom's health and stays in the scan.

- **House.** Bedroom and only full bathroom upstairs; half bath on the ground floor. Fourteen straight stairs with one rail. Poor stair lighting, loose rugs in the hall, no bars in the bathroom.
- **Support.** Family ten minutes away, daily neighbor contact, choir friend driving on Wednesdays. No paid help. Denise called the network "good but thin on weekdays".
- **Recommendations.** Eleven items, from rugs and night lights to bars, a second rail and a pendant ([[home-assessment-follow-up-checklist-2025-04]]). One longer-term point: plan now for the day the stairs are too much.
