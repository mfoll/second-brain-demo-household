# Marching band

## Summary

Overview of marching bands: instrumental groups that play while marching, historically military, with brass, woodwind and percussion and often uniforms and flags.

## Why It Matters

Read in 2025-08 when Eli joined marching band in grade 9 and we were asked for a uniform fee and a summer band camp. It explained why the season is front-loaded in August to November and why rehearsals run outdoors after school ([[band-calendar-2026-27]], [[band-fees-2026]]).

## Key Details

- Instrumentation: brass, woodwind and percussion.
- Military origins explain the uniforms and drill.
