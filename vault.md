# Brierly Household Brain

Working memory of the Brierly household, kept mainly by Nathan Brierly and read by Renata. Start here when no Project is named.

## Outcome

Nathan, Renata or an agent can resume a household task, a care arrangement or a money decision from this vault without reconstructing what was decided last time, and without the vault ever holding a medical record, an account number or a password.
