# Should We Save HSA Receipts For Later Reimbursement?

Raised 2025-11-10 with the HDHP decision; answered 2025-11-20 after Natalie Brooks confirmed the administrator's rule.

## Current Answer

Yes. Any eligible cost paid out of pocket after the HSA was opened (2026-01-01) can be reimbursed from the HSA later, with no deadline, as long as we keep the receipt and the explanation of benefits. So we pay out of pocket when it suits us and treat the receipts as a claim on the HSA.

## How we do it

- Receipts and EOB statements scanned to Paperless-ngx, tag `health` and `hsa-unreimbursed` ([[file-a-paper-document]]).
- Running total kept in the budget sheet: $2,400 of 2026 costs paid from savings, unreimbursed.
- When reimbursed, the tag changes to `hsa-reimbursed`.

## Caveat

Rules for this come from the administrator and the tax authority, not from us; [[imogen-park]] confirmed the records are what she would need if asked.
