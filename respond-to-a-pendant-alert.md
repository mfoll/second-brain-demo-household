# Respond To A Pendant Alert

What to do when the Sentrel Alert response center calls about Mom. Written 2026-01-18 for Nathan, Renata and Claire; Fern has a one-page version.

## Inputs

- The call from the response center. They will say whether Mom answered through the base unit and whether emergency services were sent.
- Phone, car keys. The lockbox code is in Bitwarden ("Mom" collection) and the response center gives it to responders.

## Steps

1. **Answer and listen.** Write down the time and what the center says. Ask: did Mom speak? Are emergency services on the way?
2. **If emergency services are on the way:** go to Mom's house (10 minutes) or, if they have already left with her, ask which hospital. Take the blue folder from Mom's desk, top drawer, which has the copy of her healthcare proxy ([[moms-care-documents-index]]).
3. **If Mom spoke and says she is fine** (accidental press, usually gardening): call her yourself. If she sounds fine to you, stop. If anything is unclear, go anyway.
4. **If you cannot go:** call the next person on the list ([[mom-care-contacts-sheet]]): Fern next door, then Renata, then Claire. Do not hang up until someone has said "I'm going".
5. **At the house:** let responders in if needed. Do not move Mom if she has fallen; wait for them.
6. **After:** text the family group what happened in one line. Nothing medical in the text or in this vault; details stay between Mom, the clinic and whoever was there.
7. **Within a day:** note the date and outcome in the Maintenance Log of [[moms-home-safety-upgrades]] or [[moms-care-plan]] if anything changed.

## Guardrails

- Never put the lockbox code, Mom's clinic login or any clinical detail in a message or note.
- If Nathan is unreachable and a decision is needed at hospital, Claire acts as successor agent ([[who-acts-for-mom-if-nathan-cannot]]).

## Verification

Test call with the center every six months, done at each [[six-month-care-plan-review]]. Last test: 2026-07-27, connected from the garden.
