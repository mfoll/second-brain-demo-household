# Should We Stay On The High-Deductible Plan?

Raised 2026-06-12 when the 2026 deductible was used up; answered 2026-10-21.

## Current Answer

> [!info]
> Not for 2027. We go back to the copay plan at $330 a month more, keep the HSA open and invested, and revisit for 2028 only if the emergency fund is back at $28,000 and has stayed there.

## Reasoning

- On total cost the HDHP wins in every scenario we modeled, by $1,500 to $4,500 a year ([[health-plan-comparison-2027]]).
- Its weakness is cash timing: up to $4,000 in a quarter in a 2026-like year. With the fund being rebuilt that would come straight out of it again.
- The condition we set in 2025, a full emergency fund, is not met ([[health-plan-decision-2025-11-10]]).

## Rejected

- Staying on the HDHP and pre-funding the deductible in the HSA: needs about $4,000 by January that would otherwise go to the [[emergency-fund-rebuild]].
- Switching dental or vision at the same time: no reason to.

## What would reopen it

The emergency fund at target, no large expected costs, and a premium gap that stays near $330 a month. See [[open-enrollment-2027]].
