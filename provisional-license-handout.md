# Provisional License Handout

The two-page handout the Wrenfield County licensing office gave Maya with her temporary license on 2026-03-18. It is the authority for the legal limits we repeated in the [[house-driving-agreement]].

## Summary

- Provisional period: 12 months from the test date, so until 2027-03-18, provided there are no at-fault crashes or moving violations. A violation restarts the 12 months.
- Night limit: no driving between 23:00 and 05:00 unless a licensed driver aged 21 or over is in the front seat, or the trip is to or from work or a school activity with a signed letter.
- Passenger limit: no more than one passenger under 21 who is not a family member.
- Phone: no handheld or hands-free phone use at all while driving during the provisional period.
- After 12 months the limits lift automatically; no second test.

## Why It Matters

Two details were stricter or looser than we assumed. The phone rule bans hands-free calls too, which settled a point in the agreement ([[distracted-driving]]). The work exception to the night limit means Maya could drive home from a late shift with a letter from her employer; we have not used it.

## Caveats

Summary in our words; the handout and the licensing office's current rules govern. Background on the general model in [[graduated-driver-licensing]].
