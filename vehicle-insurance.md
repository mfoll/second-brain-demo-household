# Vehicle insurance

## Summary

English Wikipedia article on insurance for cars and other road vehicles: protection against physical damage and injury from collisions and the liability that follows, with optional cover for theft and for damage not caused by a collision (weather, vandalism, hitting a fixed object). It notes that terms vary a great deal between jurisdictions.

## Why It Matters

Background for the insurance side of the minivan decision. The split between liability, collision and the non-collision ("comprehensive") cover is how our [[auto-insurance-policy]] is laid out, and it is why the Tessera with no collision cover and a newer SUV with it differ by about $380 a year in Ansel's rough figure ([[should-maya-drive-the-old-minivan]]).

General article; our policy terms are in the declarations page in Paperless-ngx.
