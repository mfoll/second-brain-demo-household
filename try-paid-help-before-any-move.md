# Try Paid Help Before Any Move

## Stance

Before Mom moves anywhere, try paid help at home for long enough to know whether it works: at least three months, with a review.

## Why I Think This

- A move is close to irreversible; an aide can be stopped with two weeks' notice.
- Mom asked for it in writing ([[margarets-stated-preferences-2025-07]]).
- Even at three mornings a week, paid help costs well under half of assisted living ([[housing-options-comparison]]).
- The family network is at capacity ([[informal-care-network]]).

## What Would Change My Mind

The trial review showing that the gaps are at night, not in the daytime; then hours do not fix it. Claire's argument rests on this ([[claire-email-assisted-living-case-2026-07]]), and it is the strongest point against this stance.

## Implications

Hold the [[moms-housing-decision]] until the 2026-11-02 review is in.
