# November 2025

A month of two design decisions that both looked ahead to harder years: one about money, one about the house.

## Health plan for 2026

After the assistant found an error in my worst-case scenario, the corrected comparison favored the high-deductible plan in every case, and Renata and I chose it with an HSA on 10 November ([[health-plan-decision-2025-11-10|the 2026 health plan decision]]). Renata's condition was that the emergency fund stays the backstop. The question the assistant asked that evening, what happens if a bad year lands while we are spending on something else, got too short an answer; the October 2026 review came back to it ([[open-enrollment-review-2026-10-21|2027 open enrollment review]]). We also settled how HSA receipts are kept for later reimbursement ([[should-we-save-hsa-receipts-for-later-reimbursement|HSA receipts question]]).

## Hall bathroom design

We prepared the options before Hector and Dana came on 18 November and chose the curbless shower with a linear drain, a wider door and grab-bar blocking, keeping a tub in the primary bathroom to answer Renata's resale concern ([[bathroom-design-meeting-2025-11-18|bathroom design meeting]]). The other two bidders were asked to price the same option so the comparison in [[renovation-design-and-bids|Renovation Design And Bids]] stayed fair. The open point at month end was whether our joists allow the floor recess.

## Mom's house

Not covered by a working session, but worth anchoring: Quillan's crew fitted the grab bars and second stair rail at Mom's on 22 November ([[home-safety-install-2025-11-22|home safety install]]).
