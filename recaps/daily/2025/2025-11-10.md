# Monday 10 November 2025

## Checking the health plan comparison

Before dinner I went over [[health-plan-comparison-2026]] with the assistant. It caught that my high scenario had capped both plans at their out-of-pocket maximums without adding premiums consistently; once fixed, the HDHP came out cheaper in every scenario, which surprised me. We talked through why: the premium gap plus the $1,000 seed is bigger than the difference between the two maximums. That became the [[health-plan-break-even]] note.

## What the assistant pushed back on

It asked what would happen if a bad year landed while we were spending on something else. I answered that the emergency fund covers it. In hindsight that was the right question and too short an answer; the 2026 summer proved it ([[open-enrollment-review-2026-10-21]]).

## The decision with Renata

After dinner, Renata and I chose the high-deductible plan with an HSA for 2026 ([[health-plan-decision-2025-11-10]]). Her condition, that the emergency fund stays untouched as the backstop, went into the decision note. She will submit the election on Wednesday.

## Records

Agreed with the assistant that receipts for any out-of-pocket eligible costs go to Paperless-ngx with an `hsa-unreimbursed` tag, so the HSA can pay them back later ([[should-we-save-hsa-receipts-for-later-reimbursement]]). Nothing about anyone's health goes in the vault beyond plan, cost and dates.
