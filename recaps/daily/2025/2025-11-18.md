# Tuesday 18 November 2025

## Preparing the bathroom meeting

Before Hector and Dana came over, I worked through the three bathroom options with the assistant: like-for-like tub, a low-curb shower, and a curbless shower with a [[linear-drain]]. The useful part was turning Mom's situation into requirements without writing anything medical: step-free entry, room for a shower chair, a grab bar anywhere later. That produced the option table used at the [[bathroom-design-meeting-2025-11-18]].

## The decision and the compromise

After the meeting I wrote up the outcome. Curbless shower, 34-inch door, [[wall-blocking]] in every wall, +$4,200. Renata's resale concern was not dismissed; keeping the tub in the primary bathroom answered it ([[should-the-primary-bathroom-keep-its-tub]]). We also noted that the other two bidders should price the same option so the bid comparison stays fair.

## Open

Whether the floor recess works with our joist layout. Hector will check from the basement before the drawings are revised.
