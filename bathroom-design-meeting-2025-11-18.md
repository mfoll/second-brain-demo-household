# Bathroom Design Meeting 2025-11-18

Evening meeting at our kitchen table with [[hector-quillan]] (Quillan Remodeling) and [[dana-whitlock]] (Birchwell Cabinetry), Renata and me, about the hall bathroom. This is where the curbless shower was decided.

## At a Glance

> [!success]
> We chose a curbless shower with a linear drain, a 34-inch door and blocking in every wall for future grab bars, instead of replacing the tub. It adds $4,200 to Quillan's price. Renata's resale concern was settled by keeping the tub in the primary bathroom.

## Options On The Table

| Option | Added cost | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. New tub with shower over, like for like | $0 (base bid) | Cheapest, best for resale to families with small kids | Tub edge is the hazard Mom already struggles with; no change for us later |
| B. Low-threshold shower with a 2-inch curb | +$2,100 | Simpler waterproofing, standard drain | Still a lip to step over; no rolling access |
| C. Curbless shower, linear drain, 34-inch door, full wall blocking | +$4,200 | Step-free, works with a shower chair or walker, grab bars anywhere later | Floor has to be recessed between joists; more waterproofing risk; no tub upstairs |

## What Was Decided

Option C. The $4,200 covers recessing the subfloor 3/4 inch, a 36-inch [[linear-drain]] along the back wall, a liquid membrane over the whole floor, widening the door from 28 to 34 inches, and 2x10 [[wall-blocking]] at grab bar heights in all four walls. Two grab bars go in now; the rest whenever someone needs them.

## Why

- Mom stays over several times a year (holidays, and when her house is being worked on). Since her January 2025 fall she avoids stepping over a tub edge and has been showering at her own house before visits. That is a bad arrangement for a woman of 79.
- We plan to stay in this house for at least twenty years. A bathroom that works with a cane or a walker is cheaper to build now than to retrofit at 65 ([[aging-in-place]], [[build-for-the-next-twenty-years]]).
- Hector has built four curbless showers in the last two years and named his tile setter, [[sione-tupou]].

## Renata's Concern

Renata wanted to keep a tub for resale: families with toddlers look for one. Dana said buyers usually want at least one tub in the house, not in every bathroom. Compromise: the primary bathroom keeps its tub and is not touched in this project ([[should-the-primary-bathroom-keep-its-tub]]). Renata agreed on that basis and said so in the follow-up email.
