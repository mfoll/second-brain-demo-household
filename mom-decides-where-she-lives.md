# Mom Decides Where She Lives

## Stance

> [!info]
> While Mom can understand her options and their risks, where she lives is her decision. The family's job is to give her real options with real costs, say plainly what worries us, and then support what she chooses.

## Scope

Her home, her daily routine, her helpers. Not decisions that would put other people at risk, and not a situation where she can no longer weigh the options; then the [[healthcare-proxy]] and her written wishes guide us ([[margarets-stated-preferences-2025-07]]).

## Why I Think This

- She is capable and has been consistent since 2025 ([[what-mom-has-said-about-moving]]).
- Moves made against an older person's will tend to go badly, and she would not forgive it.
- The power of attorney was signed as a backstop. Using it to steer her would break the trust it rests on.

## What Would Change My Mind

- A second fall on the stairs, or a night when she needed help and nobody could reach her for hours.
- Evidence that she no longer understands the risk.
- Mom herself asking.

## Implications

Claire disagrees on timing; her view is recorded, not overruled ([[claire-email-assisted-living-case-2026-07]]). No tours, deposits or listings without Mom's agreement ([[moms-housing-decision]]).
