# Home Care Aide Trial

A three-month trial of paid help at Mom's house, agreed on the [[family-care-call-2026-07-26]] after the day program did not work.

## Outcome

Three-month trial of a Greywillow aide on Monday and Thursday mornings, reviewed with the family on 2026-11-02.

## Current State

- Running since 2026-08-03. Aide: [[kwabena-mensah]]; scheduling: [[rosalind-tate]] at Greywillow Home Support.
- Monday and Thursday, 9:00 to 13:00, 8 hours a week at $32 an hour: $256 a week, about $1,109 a month.
- Split 60% Mom (from her pension), 20% Nathan, 20% Claire: $153.60, $51.20 and $51.20 a week ([[mom-care-cost-split-sheet]]).
- Visits so far: 23 of 24 scheduled through 2026-10-22; one Thursday (2026-09-03) cancelled by Mom for a choir outing ([[greywillow-aide-visit-log]]).
- Midpoint check-in on 2026-09-21 went well enough to keep going unchanged ([[aide-trial-midpoint-check-in-2026-09-21]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

The trial and its review only. Kwabena helps with groceries, laundry, light housekeeping, meal preparation for the next two days and going with Mom to appointments when she asks. No personal care is in the service plan; Mom declined it at intake ([[greywillow-intake-visit-2026-07-29]]). Greywillow keeps its own care notes; we see the visit log and invoices, not their notes.

## Working Context

- Why an aide and not more family time: Nathan already does Tuesday evening and Sunday; Claire is four hours away; Renata's mornings are fixed. See [[claire-and-nathan-care-roles]].
- Why Greywillow: compared with two other agencies in 2026-07 ([[home-care-agency-comparison-2026-07]]). Greywillow was not the cheapest ($32 against $29) but could promise the same aide each visit, which mattered more to Mom than $24 a week.
- Claire agreed on condition of a firm review date. Her case for assisted living is in [[claire-email-assisted-living-case-2026-07]]; the review is the point where it gets heard again.
- Mom's own view after two months: she likes Kwabena, calls him "the young man who actually listens", and wants Thursdays kept. She does not want more than two mornings yet ([[what-mom-has-said-about-moving]]).

## Active Workflows

- Weekly: Kwabena's visit log in Greywillow's family app, read by Nathan on Thursday evenings.
- Monthly: invoice to Nathan, split posted to the [[mom-care-cost-split-sheet]].
