# Manage Family Finances

The money side of the household: the monthly budget, savings and the emergency fund, the annual tax return, our insurance policies, the wills, and the paperwork system that holds all of it. It never ends; it just has busier months (January to April for taxes, June for the home policy, October for benefits).

## Scope

- Budget, sinking funds and the emergency fund, run from the [[household-budget-spreadsheet]] and the first-Sunday money meeting ([[monthly-money-meeting]]).
- The household tax return, prepared with [[imogen-park]].
- Home, auto and umbrella policies with Merriline Mutual through [[ansel-duarte]]; the health plan itself sits under [[run-family-health-admin]].
- Wills, guardianship and beneficiary designations, with [[harold-iwasaki]].
- The paperwork system: Paperless-ngx for scans, Bitwarden for credentials and numbers, the fire safe for originals. See [[household-document-archive]].
- Not here: renovation spending detail (that is [[renovation-budget-tracker]] under [[kitchen-and-bath-renovation]]) and Mom's money, which is hers; we only record her share of the aide cost.
