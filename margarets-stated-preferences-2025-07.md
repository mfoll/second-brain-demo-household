# Margaret's Stated Preferences 2025-07

A half-page Mom wrote by hand at Harold Iwasaki's suggestion, before signing her documents on 2025-07-11, so her agents would know her wishes about daily life (separate from the medical wishes in her directive). She gave Nathan and Claire a photocopy each. Typed here with her permission, wording lightly shortened.

## Summary

- "I want to stay in my house as long as it is safe, and a bit longer."
- "If I need help, I would rather pay someone than have my children give up their weekends."
- "Same person, please, not a different stranger every week."
- "Ask me first. If I can't answer, ask what I would have said."
- "Keep my garden going as long as you can, even if it's only pots."
- "Choir on Wednesdays matters more than you think."

## Why It Matters

The closest thing we have to Mom's own brief for the [[moms-care-plan]]. Quoted at the [[family-care-call-2026-07-26]] and behind [[try-paid-help-before-any-move]].
