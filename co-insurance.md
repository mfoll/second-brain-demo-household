# Co-insurance

## Summary

English Wikipedia article on co-insurance in the broad sense of sharing a risk among several parties. It then separates the uses: in health insurance, the percentage the member pays once the deductible is passed, until a stop-loss limit is reached; in property insurance, a penalty applied when a property is insured for less than a stated share of its value; and further meanings in title and liability insurance.

## Why It Matters

Looked up alongside [[copayment]] for the 2027 comparison. The health sense is our HDHP after the deductible: we pay 20%, the plan 80%, until the [[out-of-pocket-maximum]]. The property sense explained why the same word turns up in our home policy, where it means a penalty for being underinsured relative to the house's value. That was relevant when the dwelling coverage was raised for the renovation ([[home-insurance-renewal-2026-06-01]]).
