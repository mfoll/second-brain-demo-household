# Mom's Care Plan

The umbrella for keeping Mom safe and supported in the place she chooses. Started after her January 2025 fall, when it became clear that "call us if you need anything" was not a plan.

## Outcome

A written, agreed support arrangement that keeps Margaret living safely where she chooses, with legal documents in place and a review every six months.

## Current State

- Legal documents and portal access done in 2025 ([[moms-legal-documents]]). Home safety work done 2026-01-16 ([[moms-home-safety-upgrades]]).
- The [[home-care-aide-trial]] is the live piece: [[kwabena-mensah]] from Greywillow, Monday and Thursday mornings, $256 a week, review on 2026-11-02.
- Informal network unchanged: [[fern-ochoa]] texts every morning, [[lorraine-whitby]] drives her to choir on Wednesdays, Nathan visits Tuesday evenings and Sunday lunch ([[moms-weekly-support-schedule]]).
- Claire agreed to the trial but still argues for assisted living ([[family-care-call-2026-07-26]]). The "where does Mom live" question has its own project: [[moms-housing-decision]].

## Scope and Boundaries

Covers the arrangement as a whole: who helps, who pays, which documents exist and where they are, and when we review. Child projects hold the detail. Medical matters stay between Mom, [[amira-saleh]] and the clinic portal; this vault records appointments and access only ([[sensitive-records-stay-at-source]]). Mom is the decision-maker for her own life while she can be; the power of attorney is a backstop, not a steering wheel ([[mom-decides-where-she-lives]]).
