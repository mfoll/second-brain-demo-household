# Care Decisions Need A Written Review Date

## Stance

Every care change gets a review date, written down at the time it is agreed, with who attends. No open-ended arrangements.

## Why I Think This

- The adult day program had none and drifted for two months after it had clearly stopped working ([[adult-day-program-lessons]]).
- The aide trial had one, and it let Claire agree without giving up her view ([[family-care-call-2026-07-26]]).
- Needs change slowly, so nobody notices without a fixed point.

## What Would Change My Mind

If reviews became a monthly argument that tired Mom out. Six months is the default for that reason ([[six-month-care-plan-review]]).

## Implications

Every care Project gets a dated review in its Next Actions.
