## What was discussed

1. **Why the day program stopped.** Mom attended 9 of 34 planned days. She said she would rather have someone come to her than go somewhere to be "kept busy". Nobody argued with that ([[why-did-the-adult-day-program-not-work]]).
2. **Options on the table.** Nathan brought three: (a) more family visits, (b) a home care aide two mornings a week, (c) a move. Renata pointed out that (a) is what we have, and it is already at capacity on our side ([[claire-and-nathan-care-roles]]).
3. **Claire's position.** Claire made the case for [[wrenhollow-assisted-living-brochure-2026|Wrenhollow Assisted Living]], about $5,400 a month: staff at night, no stairs, meals, company. She had sent it in writing the week before ([[claire-email-assisted-living-case-2026-07]]). Her main worry is a second fall at night with nobody there.
4. **Mom's position.** She will not move now. She said she knows the stairs are a problem and wants to deal with it "when it is a problem, not before". She agreed to try an aide, provided it is the same person each time and that they help with the house, not with her.
5. **Agency.** Greywillow Home Support, on Denise's recommendation and our comparison ([[home-care-agency-comparison-2026-07]]): $32 an hour, same aide promised, two weeks' notice to change.
6. **Money.** Mom insisted on paying most of it herself. Settled on 60% Mom from her pension, 20% each for Nathan and Claire ([[how-should-moms-care-costs-be-split]]). Per week: $153.60, $51.20, $51.20.
