# Athletics Fee Notice 2025

Pellbrook High's notice on athletics participation fees for 2025-26, received 2025-09-05.

## Summary

- $150 per student per sport season, due before the first practice.
- Capped at $300 per family per year.
- Covers officials, transport to away events and facility time.
- A sports physical confirmation must be on file with the athletics office; the school holds it, not the coach.
- Paid in the parent portal.

## Why It Matters

We paid Maya's 2025-26 swim fee twice in 2025-09 (once in the portal, once by a check Renata sent with the forms). The refund took five weeks. That is why [[process-a-school-form-or-fee]] exists. Same rules apply for 2026-27.
