# Robotics Season Logistics

Working note for the parent side of Eli's robotics season: what happens each week and what changes in January.

## Fall (2026-09-08 to 2026-12-17)

- Tuesday 18:00 to 20:30: [[sunita-rao]] drives both ways.
- Thursday 18:00 to 20:30: we drive. Nathan drops at 17:50, Renata picks up at 20:30 unless working late, then Nathan.
- Eli eats before he goes; sessions have no food.
- Monday and Wednesday band rehearsals end 17:30; Eli walks home (1.2 miles).

## Build season (2027-01-09 to 2027-02-20)

- Tuesday and Thursday sessions extend to 21:00.
- Saturday 09:00 to 14:00, rotation with Sunita, us first on 2027-01-09.
- Expect about 9 hours a week of robotics plus band.
- Two Saturdays clash with band rehearsals ([[can-eli-do-band-and-robotics]]).

## Competition (2027-03-13)

- Team bus from school at 6:30, back about 20:00, per [[robotics-team-handbook-2026]].
- Renata volunteers 7:30 to 12:30 at the regional.
- Travel permission form due 2027-02-26.

## Costs so far

$475 season fee, paid 2026-09-18 ([[robotics-season-budget-2026-27]]). Expected extra: about $60 for food on competition day and a possible $40 for safety glasses and a tool kit.
