# House Purchase Inspection 2011

## Summary

The pre-purchase home inspection from when we bought the house in 2011, scanned in 2025-01 when I set up the vault. Useful mainly as a baseline: what was already old in 2011.

## Key Details

- Roof (1998) "mid-life, 10 to 15 years remaining". That window closed in 2021 to 2026, which matches [[roof-inspection-notes-2026-09]].
- Electrical: 100-amp service "adequate for current use". It stopped being adequate with the kitchen.
- Water heater (then a 2007 gas unit) and furnace (2007) both new-ish. The water heater failed in 2025; the furnace still runs.
- Note about "soft flooring near dishwasher, monitor". Nobody did. That was the rot found in 2026-07 ([[subfloor-rot-change-order-2026-07-08]]).

## Caveats

Fifteen years old. Treat as history, not condition.
