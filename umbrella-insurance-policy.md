# Umbrella Insurance Policy

## Summary

Summary of our $1 million personal umbrella policy with Merriline Mutual, renewed with the home policy each June. Bought in 2019 on [[ansel-duarte]]'s advice when we added the deck.

## Key Details

- Limit: $1 million over the auto and home liability limits.
- Premium: $310 a year until 2026; $340 from the 2026-06 renewal, with a further increase expected at the next renewal now that Maya is a listed driver.
- Requires minimum liability limits on the underlying auto and home policies; both meet them ([[auto-insurance-policy]], [[home-insurance-policy]]).
- Covers all household members, including Maya and Eli.

## Caveats

Ansel quoted $2 million at about $150 a year more. Undecided: [[should-we-raise-the-umbrella-limit]].
