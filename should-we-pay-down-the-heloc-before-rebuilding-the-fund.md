# Should We Pay Down The HELOC Before Rebuilding The Fund?

Opened 2026-07-10.

## The question

The HELOC charges about 8.25%, variable; the fund earns 3.1%. Every dollar sent to the fund instead of the HELOC costs about 5 cents a year. On $890 a month over a year, that is roughly $250 of extra interest.

## Arguments

- For paying down first: it is the better return, guaranteed.
- For the fund first: a paid-down HELOC can be drawn again, but [[ruben-castillo]] confirmed the credit union can freeze or cut a line in a downturn, which is exactly when we would need it. Cash in the fund cannot be frozen.
- Renata's position: fund first, the $250 is the price of sleeping well. Mine: split, $600 to the fund and $290 extra to the HELOC.

## Current state

Fund first, by default, while we decide. Revisit at the 2027-01 money meeting.
