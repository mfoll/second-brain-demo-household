# Copayment

## Summary

English Wikipedia article on copayments: the fixed share of a service's cost that the patient pays, with the insurer paying the rest, usually paid at the point of service.

## Why It Matters

Renata wanted a clean definition for the 2027 comparison because the Brackwater guide uses "copay" and "coinsurance" in the same table. On the copay plan a therapy or office visit is a flat $40; on the HDHP the same visit costs the full negotiated price until the deductible is met. That difference is most of what [[health-plan-comparison-2027]] is about.

## Caveats

The article's examples are drawn from one national system and include a program we have nothing to do with. Only the general definition was used.
