# Home Energy Credit Worksheet 2025

## Summary

My worksheet for the 30% national home energy credit on the heat pump water heater, prepared 2026-02-14 for [[imogen-park]].

## Key Details

- Equipment: heat pump water heater installed 2025-05-21 by [[ray-okonjo]], replacing the failed 2007 gas unit ([[water-heater-replacement-2025-05-21]]).
- Qualifying cost: $2,450 including installation labor, per the itemized invoice ([[heat-pump-water-heater-invoice-2025-05]]). No rebate received that would reduce the base.
- Credit: 30% × $2,450 = $735. Annual cap $2,000, so not binding.
- Evidence Imogen asked for: the invoice showing the efficiency rating and labor line, and the manufacturer's certification statement for the model. Both scanned to Paperless-ngx under `tax-2025`.
- Running cost: utility bills since June 2025 show water heating down by roughly half; detail in [[water-heater-energy-use-comparison]].

## Why It Matters

Template for any later claim. If we replace the furnace with a heat pump, the same credit applies, and the $2,000 cap would bind; Imogen suggested checking whether splitting work across two tax years helps.
