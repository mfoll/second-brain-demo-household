# Water Heater Replacement 2025-05-21

The 2007 gas water heater failed on Saturday 2025-05-17: water on the basement floor from a corroded tank seam, found by Eli when he went down for the camping gear. I shut off the water and gas to it and we did four days of kettle washing and showers at Mom's.

[[ray-okonjo]] came on 2025-05-18 and priced two options:

| Option | Installed | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Gas, same size | $1,750 | Could install the next day |
| Heat pump water heater, 50 gal | $2,450 | Install Wednesday; eligible for the 30% home energy credit |

After the credit the heat pump unit was about $1,715, slightly cheaper than gas, with lower running costs. Renata agreed by text within the hour. Installed 2025-05-21 ([[heat-pump-water-heater-invoice-2025-05]]).

What followed:

- [[imogen-park]] claimed $735 on the 2025 return, filed 2026-04-09 ([[tax-return-2025]]).
- The gas bill fell; my before and after numbers are in [[water-heater-energy-use-comparison]].
- It started my reading about the furnace ([[home-energy-retrofits]]).
- It added a 30-amp load to the old panel, which mattered in 2026 ([[kitchen-electrical-load-calculation]]).
- Lesson for the register: add an expected-life column and look at it every October ([[home-and-vehicle-register]]).
