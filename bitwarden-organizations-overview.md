# Organizations Overview

## Summary

Bitwarden help center page on organizations: shared spaces for passing vault items between users, created from the web app, with a free tier and paid Families, Teams and Enterprise plans. Items are shared through collections, which work like shared folders, and members can be grouped to grant collection access in bulk. Paid organizations include premium features for every member and encrypted attachment storage.

## Why It Matters

This is the page I followed in February 2025 to move from two personal vaults to one family organization. Collections map directly onto our areas ("Insurance", "Health", "Money", "House", "Kids", "Mom"), which lets Claire see the "Mom" collection without seeing anything else, and lets Maya have "Kids" only. The attachment storage is where insurance card images and current declarations pages now live. Setup in [[bitwarden-family-organization-setup]].

## Caveats

Plan names and prices change; we pay the family plan yearly. The page is vendor documentation and describes features, not how a household should structure them.
