# Mei Tanaka-Ross Review Summary 2026-02

## Summary

Four-page written summary from [[mei-tanaka-ross]] after the one-off review on 2026-02-17, received 2026-02-19. Fee $900, flat.

## Key Details

Her recommendations, in her order:

1. Update the wills and name a guardian; she called the 2012 wills "the largest unmanaged risk" in the review.
2. Keep the emergency fund at four months of core expenses, sized on what we must pay, not on total spending ([[months-of-expenses]]). At the time that was about $26,000, which we had.
3. Fund the renovation from savings plus the HELOC as planned, but do not fund overruns from the HELOC if the fund can absorb them and be rebuilt within a year.
4. Do not reduce retirement or college contributions for the renovation.
5. Treat the HDHP as fine while the fund is full; revisit if it is drawn down.
6. Consider a higher umbrella limit once Maya drives.

## Why It Matters

Points 3 and 5 turned out to be the two decisions of 2026 ([[how-should-we-fund-the-renovation-overrun]], [[open-enrollment-review-2026-10-21]]). Point 1 is still open ([[will-and-guardianship-update]]), point 6 too ([[should-we-raise-the-umbrella-limit]]).

## Caveats

She reviewed numbers we supplied; she did not see statements. Advice for our situation at that date, not general guidance.
