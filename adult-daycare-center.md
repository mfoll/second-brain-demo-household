# Adult daycare center

## Summary

Describes adult day centers as non-residential, staffed group settings that provide meals, activities and supervision for adults who need support during the day. Distinguishes a social model from a medical model and notes growing demand.

## Why It Matters

Read before enrolling Mom at the Pellbrook Senior Center ([[pellbrook-senior-center-program-agreement-2026-02]]). The Senior Center program is a social-model program, which fitted the aim (company and lunch). The article's framing of these as group settings was the part we underweighted: Mom does not like group settings ([[why-did-the-adult-day-program-not-work]]).

## Caveats

Mixed British and American spelling and examples; descriptive, not a guide to choosing one.
