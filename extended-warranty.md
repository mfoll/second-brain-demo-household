# Extended warranty

## Summary

Article on extended warranties, also sold as service contracts or maintenance plans: coverage beyond the maker's standard warranty, sold by a manufacturer, retailer or third party and usually priced as a share of the item's price. It notes that some multi-year plans overlap the first year of the manufacturer's warranty, so the real extra coverage is shorter than the headline.

## Why It Matters

The appliance store offered plans on all three Kestova appliances at checkout. The overlap point settled it for me: a "five-year" plan on top of a two-year warranty buys three years. See [[do-we-buy-extended-warranties-on-the-kestova-appliances]] and [[skip-extended-warranties]].

## Key Details

- Overlap with the standard warranty is the first thing to check in any plan.
- Plans are priced to be profitable for the seller on average, which is why self-insuring makes sense for us on items under about ,500.
