# Benefits Briefing 2026-10-08

Brackwater's open enrollment briefing, run by [[natalie-brooks]], 45 minutes online. Renata attended and sent me her notes the same evening.

## Points that mattered for us

- Plan designs unchanged for 2027; premiums up $10 a month on both medical plans ([[brackwater-benefits-guide-2027]]).
- An existing HSA stays with the employee after a switch to the copay plan; contributions stop, spending and investing continue.
- The last HSA payroll contribution of the year is the final December payroll if you change plans.
- Elections must be completed in the portal by 2026-11-14; no paper forms.
- Dependents turning 18 stay on the plan without action until 26.
