# Dana Whitlock

Kitchen and bath designer at Birchwell Cabinetry. She drew both rooms and placed the cabinet order.

## How We Work Together

Design was billed at $600 and credited against the cabinet order. Three rounds of kitchen drawings from 2025-09 to 2025-11, each with a measured plan and elevations as PDF ([[kitchen-design-drawings-birchwell]]). She is good at asking how we actually cook and where things land after grocery runs, and frank when a layout looks better than it works: she was the one who measured the 34-inch aisle that killed the island ([[kitchen-layout-option-b]]).

She sat in on the [[bathroom-design-meeting-2025-11-18]] and suggested the linear drain along the back wall rather than at the entry, so the floor slopes away from the door.
