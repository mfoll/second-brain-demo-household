# Student financial aid

## Summary

General overview of financial support for students: scholarships, grants, student loans and work-study, each with its own advantages and drawbacks.

## Why It Matters

My starting point for the money side of college in fall 2025. It gave us the vocabulary for reading aid offers: which parts are gifts and which are loans or earnings. That distinction is the whole basis of [[net-price]] and our checklist for the national aid form ([[student-aid-form-checklist]]).

## Key Details

- Four forms of aid: scholarships, grants, loans, work-study.
- Grants and scholarships do not need repaying; loans do; work-study is earned.

## Caveats

International overview; nothing about our national aid form or its deadlines, which come from the school's aid night ([[financial-aid-night-2026-10-08]]).
