# Greywillow Family App Guide

Greywillow's two-page guide for families using their app, emailed by [[rosalind-tate]] before the first visit.

## Key Details

- Family members see visit times, the aide's short visit note and invoices. Care notes for the agency stay internal.
- Access only with the client's written consent; Mom gave consent for Nathan and Claire on 2026-07-29.
- Messages to the aide go through the office, not direct.

Explains what the [[greywillow-aide-visit-log]] can and cannot show us, and why we do not copy visit notes into the vault.
