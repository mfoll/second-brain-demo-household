# How Should Mom's Care Costs Be Split?

Answered on the [[family-care-call-2026-07-26]].

## Current Answer

> [!success]
> Recurring paid care (aide, pendant) is split 60% Mom from her pension, 20% Nathan, 20% Claire. One-off work at her house is paid by Mom. Professional advice for the family, like Denise's assessment, is split 50/50 between Nathan and Claire.

## How we got there

- Mom wanted to pay everything ([[margarets-stated-preferences-2025-07]]). Claire and Nathan wanted to help and to have a say.
- Considered: 100% Mom; equal thirds; income-based between Nathan and Claire. Thirds would have taken a third of Mom's monthly margin for one service; income-based made Claire uncomfortable.
- 60/20/20 leaves Mom about $380 a month spare ([[mom-pension-and-care-affordability-2026-09]]) and keeps Nathan and Claire equal, which matters more to both of them than the exact amount.

## Revisit when

Paid hours roughly double, or any move is decided.
