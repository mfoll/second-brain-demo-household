# Net Price

What a year of college actually costs a family: the full cost of attendance (tuition, fees, housing, food, books, travel) minus grants and scholarships. Loans and work-study are not subtracted, because they are paid back or earned.

## Why we use it

Sticker prices on Maya's list range from about $24,000 to $71,000 a year; net prices from the calculators range much less, roughly $16,900 to $31,000 ([[college-cost-comparison]]). Comparing stickers would have ruled out schools that are cheaper for us than the in-county option.

## How we compute it

1. Run each college's net price calculator with the 2025 tax return figures (the form uses the return from two years before enrollment).
2. Record the estimate, the date run, and whether it includes merit aid.
3. Replace the estimate with the actual offer in the spring: offer total, minus loans, minus work-study, gives the true net price.

## Limits

Calculators are estimates and some ignore outside scholarships or assets. They are a screening tool; the decision waits for actual offers ([[compare-aid-offers-before-committing]], [[how-will-we-pay-for-mayas-first-year]]).
