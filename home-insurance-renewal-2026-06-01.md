# Home Insurance Renewal 2026-06-01

Annual review call with [[ansel-duarte]] before the home and umbrella policies renewed.

## What changed

- Dwelling coverage raised to reflect the renovation: the hall bathroom was finished on 2026-05-22 and the kitchen contract was in progress. Ansel reran the rebuild estimate with the contract value.
- Premium from $1,860 to $2,140 a year. About $190 of the increase is the higher dwelling coverage; the rest is the general rate change.
- Umbrella premium from $310 to $340.

## What we checked

- Quillan Remodeling's liability certificate on file for the kitchen phase.
- Loss-of-use cover applies if the house becomes unlivable, not for a kitchen out of service, so the temporary kitchen costs were ours.
- The roof: Merriline may move it to depreciated-value coverage after 30 years. That is 2028 for our 1998 roof; it supports getting quotes in spring 2027 ([[roof-replacement]]).
