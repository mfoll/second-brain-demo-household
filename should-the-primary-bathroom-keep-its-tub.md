# Should The Primary Bathroom Keep Its Tub?

Answered 2025-11-18: yes. It is the compromise that made the curbless shower in the hall bathroom possible.

Renata's concern at the [[bathroom-design-meeting-2025-11-18]] was resale: families with small children want a bathtub. [[dana-whitlock]] said buyers generally expect one tub in the house, not two. So the primary bathroom keeps its 1998 tub and stays out of the renovation, and the hall bathroom gets the step-free shower.

If we ever remodel the primary bathroom, this question reopens. Until then, the house has one tub and one curbless shower, which suits both a toddler-age buyer and us at 70.
