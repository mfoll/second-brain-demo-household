# Will Consultation 2025-06-24

First meeting with [[harold-iwasaki]] about our own wills, two weeks before he finished Mom's documents ([[moms-legal-documents]]). One hour at his office, Renata and me.

## What he flagged in the 2012 wills

- No trust for the children: either child could inherit outright at the age of majority.
- Guardian named with no alternate.
- Nothing about digital accounts or how an executor would get into them.

## What we agreed

- Mirror wills, everything to the surviving spouse, then to the children equally, held in trust until 25.
- A guardian and an alternate for Eli; Maya will be an adult by signing.
- Trustee and guardian can be different people.
- Review the beneficiary designations ourselves before the next meeting ([[beneficiary-designations-checklist]]).
- Digital access: he recommended a password manager's emergency access over any written list. We set it up in July ([[bitwarden-about-emergency-access]]).

## Follow-up

Engagement letter received 2025-06-27, $2,400 flat ([[iwasaki-engagement-letter-2025]]). The guardian question stalled us through the autumn; the project then paused in 2026-08.
