# Personal budget

## Summary

English Wikipedia article on personal or household budgets as plans for coordinating income and spending. It walks through several common methods: a fixed split between needs, wants and savings; paying yourself first; separate savings pots for specific goals with a monthly amount worked back from a date; cash envelopes per category; and zero-based allocation. A search for the envelope system redirects here.

## Why It Matters

Read during the [[household-budget-reset]] to compare the envelope approach we had used since 2019 ([[budget-spreadsheet-2024-envelopes]]) with a full monthly allocation. The comparison made it clear our problem was not the envelope idea but the missing annual costs, which pushed us toward [[sinking-fund]] lines. The "separate pot per goal, divided by the months left" method in the article is essentially how we size them.
