# Sinking fund

## Summary

English Wikipedia article on sinking funds in their original sense: money an organization sets aside over time to pay for a future capital expense or to repay long-term debt.

## Why It Matters

I looked up where the household-budget term came from. The original meaning, saving steadily for a known future cost, is exactly how we use it in the [[household-budget-spreadsheet]] for car repairs, travel and renewals. Our working definition is in [[sinking-fund]].

## Caveats

The article is about corporate and government finance (bonds, debt repayment); nothing in it is household-specific.
