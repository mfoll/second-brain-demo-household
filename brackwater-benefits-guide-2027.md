# Brackwater Benefits Guide 2027

## Summary

Brackwater's 2027 open enrollment guide, received 2026-10-01. Same two medical plans and the same Tessaline Health network as 2026; premiums up slightly; plan designs unchanged.

## Key Details

- Family premium, employee share: copay plan $650 a month (from $640); high-deductible plan $320 a month (from $310). The gap stays at $330 a month.
- Deductibles, coinsurance and out-of-pocket maximums unchanged from [[brackwater-benefits-guide-2026]].
- HSA employer seed stays $1,000 for family coverage on the high-deductible plan.
- An HSA opened in a previous year stays with the employee if they change plans; no new contributions are allowed while on the copay plan.
- Enrollment window 2026-10-20 to 2026-11-14. No changes allowed later in the year except for qualifying life events.
- Dental and vision in a separate section ([[brackwater-dental-and-vision-plans-2027]]).
