# Application Strategy Meeting 2026-10-11

Sunday family meeting, Maya, Renata and me, one hour after dinner, to settle the application plan after the [[financial-aid-night-2026-10-08]].

## At a Glance

> [!note]
> No binding early decision anywhere. Early action to Kestrel Bay University and Corliss College by 2026-11-01; the other six regular decision by 2027-01-15. We compare aid offers side by side in the spring before Maya commits.

## Options Considered

1. **Early decision to Kestrel Bay.** Maya's first choice after the June visit. Better admission odds, but binding: if admitted with an acceptable offer she must enroll and withdraw the others. We would lose the chance to compare offers. Rejected.
2. **Early action to two schools, regular to six.** Non-binding; results in mid-December; Maya can still compare everything in the spring. Chosen.
3. **All regular decision.** Simplest, but Maya's two early applications are ready and an early yes would lower the stress of December and January. Rejected.

Background: [[early-action-vs-early-decision]], [[early-decision]], [[early-admission]].

## Why

The deciding factor was money: the emergency fund is at $19,235, up from its $17,360 low in August, and rebuilding to $28,000 by 2027-06-30 ([[emergency-fund-rebuild]]), and the minivan decision lands in December ([[should-maya-drive-the-old-minivan]]). We cannot judge an early decision offer as "acceptable" without other offers to compare. Renata was firmest on this; Maya agreed, a bit reluctantly.
