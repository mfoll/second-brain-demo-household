# Claire And Nathan Care Roles

Who does what between the two siblings, agreed loosely in 2025 and written down after the [[family-care-call-2026-07-26]].

**Nathan (ten minutes away):** weekly visits, Sunday lunch, emergencies, the aide contract and invoices, the portal, the cost split sheet, the vault. Agent on the legal documents.

**Claire (four hours away):** monthly family call, a weekend visit every six weeks, hosting Mom during our trips, reading the visit log, research on options (she found Wrenhollow). Successor agent.

**Renata:** midweek call, backup driver, and the person who notices when Nathan is doing too much. In 2026-06, during the kitchen demolition, he was ([[caregiver-burden]]).

**Shared:** costs at 20% each; every decision about Mom's housing.

The imbalance in hours is real and accepted; Claire balances it with hosting and research, and both prefer that to splitting tasks evenly by the clock.
