# Building insulation

## Summary

Article on building insulation: material that slows the flow of heat through a building's envelope. Thermal performance is the main goal, but insulation also serves for sound, fire and impact, and one product often does several jobs.

## Why It Matters

Read in winter while thinking about the furnace. Insulating the attic first would shrink the heat load and so the size and cost of any heat pump ([[heat-pump-payback]]). Part of [[home-energy-retrofits]].

## Key Details

- The article is general; it does not give values for our climate.
- Our attic has the original 1998 batts; nobody has measured their depth since. A step in the fall checklist now.
