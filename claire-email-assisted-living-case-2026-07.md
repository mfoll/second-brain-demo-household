# Claire Email Assisted Living Case 2026-07

Claire's email of 2026-07-19 to Nathan and Renata, sent the day she drove Mom home after the Halvane week. Summarized; the email stays in Nathan's mailbox.

## Summary

Claire's case for Wrenhollow, in her points:

1. The stairs. Mom manages them now, but the bedroom and full bathroom are upstairs, and the risk is at night.
2. Nights alone. A pendant helps only if she presses it.
3. Nine days at Claire's showed that Mom enjoys company at meals more than she admits.
4. Waiting for a crisis means moving in a hurry to whatever has a bed.
5. Money: with the house sold, Wrenhollow is affordable for about eight years.

She asked that the family call look at Wrenhollow seriously and not only at "more help at home".

## Why It Matters

The strongest written version of the dissent recorded on the [[family-care-call-2026-07-26]]. It deserves an answer at the review and in [[should-mom-stay-in-her-house]], especially point 4.

## Caveats

Claire's view, written the day after a long visit. Mom read it and disagreed with point 3.
