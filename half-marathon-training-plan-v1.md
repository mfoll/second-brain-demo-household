# Half Marathon Training Plan v1

> [!warning]
> Superseded on 2026-02-02 by [[half-marathon-training-plan-v2]] after two weeks of ice in January. Kept to show why version 2 has a flexible day.

The first 16-week plan for [[half-marathon-2026]], from 2026-01-12 to race day 2026-05-03. Adapted from a free beginner-intermediate plan with [[calvin-odom]]'s interval sessions added.

## Summary

- Four runs a week: Tuesday intervals, Thursday tempo, Saturday easy, Sunday long.
- Weekly mileage 18 rising to 34; long run 6 rising to 12 miles; three cut-back weeks.
- No spare day: a missed run was simply lost.

## What Went Wrong

Weeks 2 and 3 (from 2026-01-19) were lost to ice on the river path and roads. The plan had no way to absorb that, and catching up would have meant jumping from 12 to 22 miles a week.
