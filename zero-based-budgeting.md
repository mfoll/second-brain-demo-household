# Zero-based budgeting

## Summary

English Wikipedia article on zero-based budgeting as an organizational method from the 1970s: each budget period starts from nothing and every expense has to be justified again, rather than carried forward from last year. It discusses the savings it can find and the effort of rebuilding the budget every cycle.

## Why It Matters

I found it when looking for a name for what Renata described in January 2025. We borrowed the starting-from-zero idea, not the full method: our [[household-budget-spreadsheet]] assigns every dollar each month, but categories carry over and only get re-justified once a year. The article's warning about the cost of rebuilding every cycle is why we did not do the full version ([[household-budget-reset]]).

## Caveats

The article is about organizations and governments. Household "zero-based budgets" in personal finance writing mean something looser.
