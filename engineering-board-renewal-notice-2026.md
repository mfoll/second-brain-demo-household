# Engineering Board Renewal Notice 2026

The licensing board's renewal reminder letter, received 2026-09-08 by post and email.

## Summary

- License expires 2026-12-31; renewal opens online 2026-11-01.
- Requirement for the cycle: 30 professional development hours, at least 2 in professional ethics ([[professional-development-hours]]).
- Fee $180 if renewed by 2026-12-31; $90 late fee in January; license lapses after 2027-01-31.
- The engineer attests the hours on the form; certificates are not uploaded but must be kept for six years and produced if audited.
- About 5% of renewals are audited each cycle.

## Why It Matters

Sets the deadline and the evidence rule for [[pe-license-renewal]]. The six-year retention is why certificates go to Paperless-ngx rather than staying in email.
