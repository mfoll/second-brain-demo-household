# Power of attorney

## Summary

Explains a power of attorney as a written authorization for one person (the agent or attorney-in-fact) to act for another (the principal) in private, business or legal matters, with variations between legal systems.

## Why It Matters

Background reading before the first meeting with [[harold-iwasaki]]. It gave us the vocabulary (principal, agent, durable) so that the meeting could be about Mom's choices rather than definitions. The durable and springing distinction became [[springing-versus-immediate-authority]].

## Caveats

General and international; the rules that actually applied to Mom came from Harold, not from here.
