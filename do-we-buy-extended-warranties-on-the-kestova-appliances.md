# Do We Buy Extended Warranties On The Kestova Appliances?

Answered 2026-06-18 at the store: no. Rechecked 2026-10-19 after [[marisol-vega]]'s visit, still no.

The offer: $689 for "five years" on all three appliances. The maker's warranty already covers two years (sealed system on the refrigerator for five), so the plan bought about three extra years on parts and labor ([[extended-warranty]]).

- $689 is 11% of the $6,240 we paid.
- Our register shows three appliance repairs in fifteen years, total about $560 ([[home-and-vehicle-register]]).
- The cooktop glass, the most likely expensive damage, is excluded from the plan anyway ([[kestova-range-manual]]).

Instead: register all three within 30 days of delivery (by 2026-11-08), and the $689 went into the emergency fund. General stance in [[skip-extended-warranties]].
