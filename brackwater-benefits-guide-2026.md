# Brackwater Benefits Guide 2026

## Summary

Brackwater Utilities' 2026 open enrollment guide for employees, 38 pages, received by Renata on 2025-10-20. It sets out the two medical plans (copay plan and high-deductible plan with HSA), both administered by Tessaline Health on the same provider network, plus dental, vision, life and disability options.

## Key Details

- Family premium, employee share: copay plan $640 a month; high-deductible plan $310 a month.
- High-deductible plan: family deductible $4,000, then 20% coinsurance to an $8,000 family out-of-pocket maximum. Preventive care covered before the deductible.
- Copay plan: family deductible $1,000, $40 office and therapy visit copays, $6,000 family out-of-pocket maximum.
- HSA: $1,000 employer seed for family coverage, paid with the first January payroll; employee contributions by payroll deduction.
- Enrollment window 2025-10-27 to 2025-11-14.
