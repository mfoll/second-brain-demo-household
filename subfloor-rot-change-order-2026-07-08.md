# Subfloor Rot Change Order 2026-07-08

Change order 1, signed 2026-07-08: replace rotten subfloor and two joist ends under the old dishwasher and sink, $3,850.

## What Happened

Hector called at lunch on 2026-07-07: with the dishwasher out, the plywood subfloor was soft across about 30 square feet and two joist ends were damaged from a slow leak that had probably been going for years. The 2011 inspection had flagged "soft flooring near dishwasher" ([[house-purchase-inspection-2011]]).

## Options

- Sister the two joists and replace the plywood in the damaged area: $3,850.
- Same, plus a new layer of plywood over the whole kitchen for a flat base: $5,300. Hector said the rest was sound and it was not needed.

We took the first. Photos in [[renovation-photo-log]]. Inspected with framing on 2026-07-21.

## Effect

Inside the "concealed conditions" clause, so outside the fixed price ([[quillan-remodeling-contract]]). With this, the plan had already used more than its whole $2,600 other line. That evening Renata and I started on how to pay for the overrun; we settled on the emergency fund rather than a second HELOC draw on 2026-08-12 ([[how-should-we-fund-the-renovation-overrun]]).
