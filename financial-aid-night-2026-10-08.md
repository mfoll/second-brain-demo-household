# Financial Aid Night 2026-10-08

Pellbrook High's information evening on paying for college, run by [[janelle-forbes]] with an aid officer from Wrenfield State University. 18:30 to 20:00 in the library. Renata and I went; Maya had swim conditioning.

What we took away:

- The national student aid form uses the tax return from two years before enrollment, so for fall 2027 it is our 2025 return ([[tax-return-2025]]). We have it.
- File early: some colleges' institutional aid is first come, first served. Target 2026-11-15.
- Compare offers on [[net-price]], not on the size of the "award": loans in an offer are still debt.
- Most of the eight colleges need a second, college-specific aid form as well; deadlines vary from 2026-11-15 to 2027-02-15. Added to [[maya-college-list]].
- Outside scholarships: the school keeps a list; local ones open in January ([[scholarship]]).

Action: Renata leads the aid form; I gather figures. Checklist in [[student-aid-form-checklist]]. This also fed [[compare-aid-offers-before-committing]].
