# Running Routine After The Half

What running looks like without a goal race, written 2026-06-07 so I can tell later whether I kept it.

- Three runs a week, about 15 miles total.
- Tuesday 6:15: intervals with [[calvin-odom]] on the river path, 5 to 6 miles including warm-up.
- Saturday 7:00: easy 4 to 5 miles with Calvin.
- Sunday: 5 to 6 miles alone, any time before 9:00.
- No Sunday long run over 7 miles until a new goal race exists.

Kept so far: June to September averaged 14.2 miles a week ([[running-log-2026]]). Missed weeks: the [[halvane-peaks-trip-2026]] (hiking instead, fine) and two weeks in August with the kitchen work.
