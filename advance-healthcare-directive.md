# Advance healthcare directive

## Summary

Explains an advance directive (also called a living will) as a document in which a person sets out what should happen with their care if they become unable to decide, and notes that its legal force differs between countries.

## Why It Matters

Mom asked about it after her first meeting with [[harold-iwasaki]], and signed one with the other documents on 2025-07-11 ([[legal-documents-signing-2025-07-11]]). Its content is hers; the vault only records where the copies are ([[moms-care-documents-index]]).

## Caveats

Legal status varies; Harold covered how it works for Mom.
