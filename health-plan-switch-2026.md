# Health Plan Switch 2026

Move the family from Brackwater's copay plan to the high-deductible plan with a health savings account for 2026.

## Outcome

Reached 2026-01-01: family enrolled in the high-deductible plan, HSA opened with Tessaline Health, $1,000 employer seed deposited in the first January payroll, payroll contribution of $300 a month from February.

## Current State

- Completed. The plan ran for 2026 and is being reversed for 2027 by [[open-enrollment-2027]].
- What happened in the plan year is recorded in [[deductible-met-2026-06-12]] and [[hsa-account-summary-2026-10]].

## Scope and Boundaries

The 2026 election only. The later reversal and its reasoning live in the 2027 project, so this note stays an honest record of what we believed in November 2025.

## Working Context

The case in 2025, from [[health-plan-comparison-2026]]: premiums $3,960 a year lower (our share $310 a month instead of $640), the $1,000 employer seed, and a family deductible of $4,000 with a family out-of-pocket maximum of $8,000. In 2024 and 2025 our claims were low; the comparison showed the HDHP cheaper in every scenario, even the worst case, because the premium gap and the seed outweigh the $2,000 difference in out-of-pocket maximums. The risk was cash demanded early in a bad year, and we accepted it because the emergency fund was $26,000 at the time.

Renata ran the election; [[natalie-brooks]] answered the question about the seed timing (paid with the first January payroll, not at enrollment). We did not move dental or vision.
