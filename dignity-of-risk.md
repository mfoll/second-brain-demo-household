# Dignity Of Risk

The idea that an adult has the right to make choices that carry some risk, and that removing every risk can take away more than it protects. [[denise-farrow]] used the phrase in 2025, explaining why she would not push Mom on the shower chair.

How we use it:

- Mom declined a raised toilet seat and a shower chair ([[home-assessment-follow-up-checklist-2025-04]]), declined personal care from Greywillow, does not wear the pendant every day and does not want to move. All recorded as her choices, not as problems to solve.
- It does not mean doing nothing. It means offering, explaining the risk plainly once, and then respecting the answer.
- It has limits. If Mom could no longer understand a risk, the [[healthcare-proxy]] and family judgment come in. We are not there.

Claire thinks we lean too far on this. That disagreement is part of [[should-mom-stay-in-her-house]].
