# Asphalt shingle

## Summary

Article on asphalt shingles, a wall or roof covering waterproofed with asphalt. Low upfront cost and easy installation make them one of the most common roofing materials in North America. The article covers types, including three-tab and the heavier architectural or laminated shingles, and how they age.

## Why It Matters

Read the day after the roofer's notes ([[roof-inspection-notes-2026-09]]). Our roof is the original 1998 three-tab. It explained what the curling and granule loss mean and why the quotes will likely be for architectural shingles. Feeds [[when-should-we-replace-the-roof]].

## Key Details

- Granule loss and curling are signs of age, not by themselves a leak.
- Laminated shingles are heavier and generally rated for longer life than three-tab.
