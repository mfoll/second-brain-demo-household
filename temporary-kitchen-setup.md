# Temporary Kitchen Setup

How we cooked from 2026-06-13 to 2026-10-18 in the dining room and basement. Written up in case we ever do this again or lend the gear.

- Dining room: refrigerator moved from the kitchen on a dolly, a microwave, a two-burner induction plate ($90), electric kettle, a toaster oven and a folding table.
- Dishes: basement laundry sink with a dish rack on the dryer. Paper plates for the first two weeks, then we gave up on them.
- Grill on the deck for most dinners in summer.
- Spend: about $420 on the plate, a utility shelf, a dish rack and paper goods, inside the "temporary kitchen and other" line ([[renovation-budget-tracker]]).
- Takeout budget raised to $60 a week for the duration.

What helped: a meal plan by the week, with two grill nights and one takeout night fixed. What did not: the old fridge was too loud in the dining room. Planned for 5 weeks, lasted 18 because of the cabinet slip.
