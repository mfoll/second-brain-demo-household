# About Emergency Access

## Summary

Bitwarden help center page on emergency access: an account holder names trusted contacts in advance; a contact can request access when needed; access is granted when the holder approves or when a waiting period set by the holder runs out. Access is either view-only or takeover, where the contact sets a new master password and gains full control. Available to premium users, including members of paid family organizations.

## Why It Matters

Set up after the 2025 will consultation, when Harold asked how an executor would get into our accounts. Renata and I are each other's emergency contact with takeover access and a 7-day wait; my sister Claire has view access to my account with a 14-day wait as a second line. This is the piece that makes [[if-something-happens-to-us]] workable without writing any password down.

## Caveats

Takeover removes the account's two-step login methods, which is the right trade-off for a death or incapacity but not for a forgotten password. The wait time is the only protection against a mistaken request, so we kept it at a week.
