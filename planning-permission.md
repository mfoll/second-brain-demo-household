# Planning permission

## Summary

General article on the approval some jurisdictions require before building, extending or sometimes demolishing. The English term "building permit" redirects here. Rules differ widely by place; the article is a survey, not a guide to any one system.

## Why It Matters

Read while comparing bids, because Birchline's bid left the permit to us and the other two included it. It helped me understand why a permit matters beyond the fee: inspections at each stage are the only independent check on hidden work like waterproofing and wiring. Our actual permit is [[building-permit-kitchen-and-bath]].

## Key Details

- Approval and inspection are separate steps; the inspection schedule is what actually protects us.
- Local rules govern everything specific, so for Pellbrook I relied on the building department's checklist and on [[walt-ferreira]], not on the article.
